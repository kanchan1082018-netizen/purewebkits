How Philippine SSS, PhilHealth & Pag-IBIG Statutory Contributions Work

### Why Philippine Contribution Calculators Are Essential for Employees and HR Professionals

Working in the Philippines requires statutory monthly deductions from employee salaries for three major government agencies: **SSS (Social Security System)** for social security and disability benefits, **PhilHealth** for national health insurance, and **Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF)** for housing savings and loans. Calculating these mandatory contributions correctly ensures payroll compliance and accurate net take-home pay computations. A Philippines SSS, PhilHealth & Pag-IBIG calculator computes employee and employer shares instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates statutory deductions locally in client memory without server uploads.

### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

Contributions follow official government schedules (SSS 4.5% employee share, PhilHealth 2.5% shared equally, Pag-IBIG 1% or 2%):

SSS Employee = Salary * 4.5%  |  PhilHealth Employee = (Salary * 5%) / 2  |  Pag-IBIG = Min(Salary * 2%, 200)

Worked Example: Earning a monthly basic salary of ₱25,000 results in an SSS employee contribution of **₱1,125.00**, a PhilHealth employee share of **₱625.00**, and a Pag-IBIG contribution of **₱200.00**, totaling **₱1,950.00 in deductions** and leaving a **Net Salary of ₱23,050.00**.
