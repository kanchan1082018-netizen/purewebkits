How Legal Bates Stamping, Zero-Padding & PDF Coordinate Placement Work

Why Law Firms, Courts & Researchers Rely on Sequential Page and Bates Numbering

When compiling merged contracts, discovery exhibits, medical records, or academic appendices into a single PDF packet, unnumbered pages make cross-referencing nearly impossible during hearings, audits, or peer reviews. In legal litigation and regulatory compliance, Bates numbering (named after Edwin G. Bates' 19th-century automatic numbering machine) assigns a unique, zero-padded alphanumeric identifier—such as EXHIBIT-A-0001 through EXHIBIT-A-0250—to every single page across an entire production set. Using pdf-lib vector typography directly in your browser, our tool stamps crisp, searchable Helvetica text at exact margin coordinates while preserving 100% of your underlying document resolution.

Bates Zero-Padding & PDF Point Coordinate Formulas + Worked Example

PDF pages measure coordinates in typographic points (72 points = 1 inch) starting from the bottom-left origin (0, 0):

Bates Label = Prefix + padStart( Start + k, Digits, "0" )  |  Right Margin X = PageWidth − TextWidth − 28 pt

Worked Example: Suppose you stamp a 12-page discovery exhibit with prefix DEF-, Start Number 1, and 4 Zero Digits in the Bottom Right corner (28 pt margin).

Page 1 receives DEF-0001, Page 2 receives DEF-0002, and Page 12 receives DEF-0012.

Because the stamps are embedded as native vector text rather than rasterized pixels, opposing counsel can search "DEF-0012" directly with Ctrl + F.
