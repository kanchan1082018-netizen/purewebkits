How Hijri (Islamic) and Gregorian Calendar Conversions Work

### Why Hijri Date Converters Are Essential for Muslims Worldwide

The **Hijri calendar** is a lunar calendar consisting of 12 lunar months in a year of 354 or 355 days. Because it is shorter than the solar Gregorian calendar by about 11 days, Islamic months migrate through all seasons over a 33-year cycle. Accurately converting dates between the Hijri and Gregorian calendars is vital for determining sacred Islamic occasions such as Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, and Hajj. A Hijri to Gregorian date converter translates dates seamlessly between both calendars. Our browser-based tool calculates conversions locally in client memory without server uploads.

### Conversion Formula + Worked Example

Hijri-to-Gregorian conversion utilizes Julian Day Number (JDN) algorithms approximating lunar and solar year ratios:

Gregorian Year ≈ Hijri Year * 0.97022 + 621.577  |  JDN = Julian Day Calculation Algorithm

Worked Example: Converting 1 Ramadan 1447 AH yields a corresponding Gregorian date of **February 19, 2026**, accurately reflecting astronomical lunar calculations.
