How Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Economics & Range Estimation Work

### Why EV Charging Cost and Range Calculators Are Essential for Drivers

Transitioning to an electric vehicle (EV) offers massive savings on fuel compared to traditional gasoline cars, but calculating precise charging costs depends heavily on local electricity rates, battery capacity, and vehicle efficiency. An EV charging cost and range calculator helps owners estimate exactly how much a full or partial charge will cost and how far their vehicle can travel. Our browser-based tool computes these metrics instantly in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate EV Charging Expenses in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Battery Capacity: Type your EV's total battery size in kilowatt-hours (kWh) into the input box.
  2. Set Electricity Rate: Enter your local home or public charging electricity cost per kWh.
  3. Input Vehicle Efficiency: Provide your energy consumption rate (kWh per 100 km or miles) to view total driving range and cost per distance immediately.
### EV Cost & Range Calculation Formula + Worked Example

EV charging expenses factor in charging efficiency losses, while range is determined by dividing available energy by energy consumption rates:

Full Charge Cost = (Battery Capacity * Rate) * 1.10 (10% Loss)  |  Range = (Battery * (100 - Charge) / 100) / Efficiency * 100

Worked Example: For a 60 kWh battery at $0.15/kWh with 16 kWh/100km efficiency, a full charge costs ~$9.75, delivering 375 km of total range.
