How Dividend Reinvestment Plans (DRIP), Yield Compounding & Wealth Building Work

### Why DRIP Calculators Are Essential for Dividend Investors

Investing in dividend-paying stocks and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) is a proven strategy for generating steady passive income. However, taking those dividend payouts as cash instead of reinvesting them leaves massive compounding potential on the table. A **Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP)** automatically uses cash dividends received to purchase additional shares (or fractional shares) of the same company without transaction fees. A **DRIP calculator** computes compounded share counts and final portfolio valuations over multi-year horizons. Our browser-based tool calculates investment simulations locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate DRIP Returns in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Initial Investment: Type your starting portfolio capital and initial stock price into the input fields.
  2. Specify Yield & Growth: Input your expected annual dividend yield percentage and annual stock price growth rate.
  3. Set Investment Duration: Specify your holding period in years to inspect your total accumulated shares and final portfolio value immediately.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

DRIP simulations compound share ownership annually as dividends purchase new shares at current stock prices:

Annual Dividend = Shares * Current Price * Dividend Yield  |  New Shares Purchased = Annual Dividend / Current Price

Worked Example: Investing $100,000 at $100 per share (1,000 shares) with a 4% dividend yield and 6% annual stock price growth over 10 years with DRIP enabled results in **1,542 total shares**, accumulating **$74,820 in reinvested dividends** and a final portfolio value of **$276,400**.
