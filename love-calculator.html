How Multi-Factor Numerology & Zodiac Compatibility Is Evaluated

Rather than relying on arbitrary random numbers that change every time you refresh the page, this Love Compatibility Calculator evaluates deterministic, transparent pairings across three classical symbolic frameworks: Pythagorean Name Numerology (comparing each partner's Expression / Destiny Number from all letters and Soul Urge Number from vowels), Birth-Date Life Path Numerology (comparing reduced birth dates 1–9 and Master Numbers 11, 22, 33), and Western Zodiac Element Harmony (comparing classical Fire, Earth, Air, and Water affinities). Because every score is computed deterministically from your actual names and birth dates, entering the same pair always yields the exact same reproducible breakdown—and swapping Partner 1 and Partner 2 preserves symmetry.
