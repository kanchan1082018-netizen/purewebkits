### Why Device Mockup Generators Are Essential for Developers, Marketers, and Product Designers

Showcasing software applications, landing pages, mobile apps, or digital portfolios requires presenting raw screenshots within professional device frames and aesthetic backgrounds rather than plain rectangular crops. High-quality mockups instantly elevate visual presentation across social media posts, pitch decks, product landing pages, and Product Hunt launches. Manually building browser windows, drop shadows, and gradient backdrops in professional vector graphic editors takes valuable time. A professional **Device Screenshot Mockup Generator** enables developers, indie hackers, and marketers to upload raw app screenshots, frame them inside sleek macOS-style browser windows or minimalist borders, set custom gradient backgrounds, and export presentation-ready graphics instantly. Our browser-based utility renders all composition frames 100% locally inside client memory using HTML5 canvas rendering contexts, ensuring absolute data privacy, zero server storage, and lightning-fast execution speed.

### Canvas Composition & Shadow Padding Formula + Worked Example

Screenshot mockups compute bounding boxes by adding padding, border radiuses, and shadow offsets around source images:

$\text{Canvas Width} = \text{Image Width} + (2 \times \text{Padding})  |  \text{Canvas Height} = \text{Image Height} + \text{Header Height} + (2 \times \text{Padding})$

Worked Example: Wrapping an **800 × 500 pixel** screenshot inside a macOS browser frame with a **40-pixel top title bar** and **80-pixel padding** on a high-resolution gradient canvas generates a polished **960 × 660 pixel** presentation mockup ready for social media.
