Complete User Guide to Crypto Profit & Loss (PnL), ROI %, Trading Fees & Break-Even Price

Why Trading Fees and Break-Even Price Matter in Crypto Trades

Calculating a cryptocurrency trade's true performance requires looking past raw price movement alone. Both entry and exit orders on spot or margin venues can incur percentage trading fees (maker or taker fees), which reduce net proceeds on a winning trade and increase net loss on a losing trade. Computing gross PnL, total fees, net PnL, Return on Investment (ROI %), and the exact break-even exit price needed to cover both entry and exit fees gives you an honest picture of trade outcomes. A browser-based Crypto Profit & Loss Calculator runs these calculations 100% locally in your browser using JavaScript—with zero server uploads.

Net PnL, ROI % & Break-Even Price Formulas

For quantity Q, entry price P_in, exit price P_out, entry fee rate f_in = Fee_in% / 100, and exit fee rate f_out = Fee_out% / 100:

Buy / Long: Gross = (P_out − P_in) × Q • Fees = P_in·Q·f_in + P_out·Q·f_out • Break-Even = P_in × (1 + f_in) / (1 − f_out)
Sell / Short: Gross = (P_in − P_out) × Q • Fees = P_in·Q·f_in + P_out·Q·f_out • Break-Even = P_in × (1 − f_in) / (1 + f_out)

Helpful Tips When Tracking Crypto Trades

  • Include Both Entry and Exit Fees: Even a small 0.10% fee on both sides adds up over frequent trades and shifts the break-even exit price slightly above entry.
  • Track Taxes Separately by Jurisdiction: Capital gains rules, holding periods, and reporting requirements vary by country.
  • Educational Math Tool Only: This calculator is for arithmetic and trade modeling and does not constitute financial or investment advice.
