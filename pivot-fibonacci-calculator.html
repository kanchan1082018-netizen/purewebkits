How Pivot Points, Support & Resistance, and Fibonacci Retracements Work

### Why Technical Analysis Calculators Are Essential for Traders

In financial markets including stocks, forex, and cryptocurrencies, technical analysis relies on mathematical indicators to identify potential trend reversals, entry points, and price targets. **Pivot Points** use the previous session's high, low, and close prices to establish objective support and resistance barriers for the upcoming trading day. Simultaneously, **Fibonacci Retracements** apply mathematical ratios (derived from the Fibonacci sequence) to measure how far a market retraces before resuming its primary trend. A pivot point and fibonacci retracement calculator computes these critical trading levels instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates technical indicators locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Pivot Points & Fib Levels in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Price Data: Input the previous session's High, Low, and Close prices for the target asset.
  2. Select Trend Direction: Choose whether your Fibonacci retracement spans an Uptrend (measuring from Low to High) or Downtrend (measuring from High to Low).
  3. Review Levels: Instantly inspect standard pivot points (PP, R1, R2, S1, S2) and key Fibonacci retracement zones (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%).
### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

Standard pivot points average high, low, and close prices, while Fibonacci levels divide price ranges by golden ratios:

Pivot Point (PP) = (High + Low + Close) / 3  |  R1 = (2 * PP) - Low  |  S1 = (2 * PP) - High

Worked Example: With a High of $105.50, Low of $98.20, and Close of $102.80, the standard Pivot Point equals **$102.17**, with Resistance 1 at **$106.40** and Support 1 at **$99.10**, while the 61.8% Fibonacci retracement level sits at **$100.99**.
