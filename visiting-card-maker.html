How Standard Visiting Card Dimensions, 300 DPI Resolution & vCard QR Codes Work

A well-crafted visiting card (business card) remains one of the fastest ways to leave a tangible, memorable impression at client meetings, trade shows, clinics, retail stores, and networking events. Standard North American and Indian visiting cards measure 3.5 × 2.0 inches (88.9 × 50.8 mm), which renders at 1050 × 600 pixels when exported at crisp 300 DPI print resolution. Pairing clean typography and clear visual hierarchy with a scannable vCard QR code allows recipients to save your phone number, email, company, and website directly into their smartphone contacts with a single camera scan. With 18 distinct templates spanning executive corporate, luxury gold/dark, creative split-panel, medical/legal, real estate, tech startup, and minimal boutique themes—plus full customization of colors, fonts, logo upload, and front/back layouts—you can tailor every detail to match your brand. A browser-based Visiting Card Maker renders everything 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

Print Resolution & Bleed Sizing Formula

Converting physical card inches to print-ready pixel dimensions at 300 DPI:

Pixel Width = 3.5 in × 300 DPI = 1050 px  |  Pixel Height = 2.0 in × 300 DPI = 600 px
Aspect Ratio = 1.75 : 1 (Standard Business Card Proportion)

Helpful Tips for Print-Ready Visiting Cards

  • Maintain Strong Text-to-Background Contrast: Light backgrounds with dark text (or deep navy/charcoal cards with crisp white and gold/cyan accents) stay legible under both warm indoor lighting and daylight.
  • Keep Essential Contact Lines Concise: Prioritizing one primary phone number, one email, your website, and a short city/street address prevents crowding on a 3.5 × 2.0 in card.
  • Test Scan Your QR Code Before Bulk Printing: Point your phone camera at the live card preview on screen to confirm your vCard contact details scan cleanly before sending files to a print shop.
