How Pregnancy Due Dates, Gestational Age, and Trimester Calculations Work

### Why Pregnancy Due Date Calculators Are Essential for Expecting Parents

Tracking fetal development, scheduling prenatal doctor appointments, and preparing for childbirth require an accurate estimation of a baby's arrival date. The standard medical method for calculating pregnancy duration is **Naegele's Rule**, which estimates the expected date of confinement (EDD) by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of the last menstrual period (**LMP**). Additionally, tracking **gestational age** in weeks and days helps obstetricians monitor fetal growth across the three distinct **trimesters**. A Pregnancy Due Date & Trimester Calculator allows expecting mothers and families to compute expected due dates, current weeks pregnant, and trimester milestones instantly. Our browser-based utility calculates pregnancy timelines locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Pregnancy Due Date in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select LMP Date: Choose the first day of your last menstrual period using the date picker.
  2. Review Due Date: Instantly inspect your estimated delivery date (EDD) calculated across 280 days.
  3. Check Trimester Status: View your current gestational age in weeks and active pregnancy trimester.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Naegele's Rule adds 280 days to the last menstrual period, while gestational age measures elapsed days from LMP:

Due Date = LMP Date + 280 Days (40 Weeks)  |  Gestational Age = Current Date - LMP Date

Worked Example: Starting with an LMP date of August 15, 2026, adds 280 days to arrive at an estimated due date of **May 22, 2027**, accurately mapping current gestational progress.
