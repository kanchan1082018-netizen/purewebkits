Complete User Guide to International Shoe & Clothing Size Conversions (US, UK, EU & CM)

Why Sizing Standards Differ Across Regions and Brands

Shopping across brands or international storefronts quickly reveals that a single size label does not mean the same thing everywhere. Footwear scales use different baselines and step intervals—such as US Men's, US Women's (typically about 1.5 sizes higher than US Men's for the same foot length), UK (typically about 1 size lower than US Men's), EU (based on Paris points), and centimeters (cm / Mondopoint). Apparel sizes similarly vary between XS–XXL letter labels, US numeric sizes, UK numeric sizes, and EU numeric sizes. A browser-based Shoe & Clothing Size Converter lets you cross-check equivalent sizes across systems immediately—100% locally in your browser with zero server uploads.

General Cross-System Sizing Relationships

While exact lasts and cuts vary by manufacturer, general cross-reference relationships follow:

US Women's Shoe ≈ US Men's Shoe + 1.5  |  UK Shoe ≈ US Men's Shoe − 1.0
Standard size charts provide approximate equivalents — always verify brand-specific measurements

Helpful Fit Tips When Buying Shoes or Clothes Online

  • Measure Both Feet Later in the Day: Feet can swell slightly by afternoon or evening, and one foot is often slightly longer than the other—size for the larger foot.
  • Check Brand Cut, Width & Stretch: Athletic sneakers, dress shoes, tailored jackets, and knitwear fit differently even at the same nominal size.
  • Use CM / Garment Measurements When Available: Foot length in centimeters and chest/waist/hip measurements give more reliable guidance than labels alone.

Worked Example: Selecting US Men's 9 on our reference shoe chart corresponds to roughly US Women's 10.5, UK 8, EU 42, and an approximate foot length of 26.5 cm.
