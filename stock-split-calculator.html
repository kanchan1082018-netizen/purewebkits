How Stock Splits, Bonus Share Issuances & Equity Adjustments Work

### Why Stock Split & Bonus Share Calculators Are Essential for Investors

Corporate actions such as **stock splits** and **bonus share issues** alter the number of shares an investor holds without directly changing their total equity investment value on the ex-date. A stock split divides existing shares into multiple new shares to improve market liquidity and affordability, while bonus shares are free additional shares issued to existing shareholders out of accumulated earnings or reserves. A stock split & bonus share calculator computes adjusted share quantities and post-action share prices instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates corporate action adjustments locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Stock Splits and Bonus Shares in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Initial Holdings: Type your current share count and purchase share price into the input fields.
  2. Select Split Ratio: Choose your stock split multiplier (e.g., 2-for-1 or 5-for-1) from the dropdown menu.
  3. Select Bonus Ratio: Choose your bonus share allocation ratio (e.g., 1-for-1 or 1-for-2) to inspect your final adjusted share count and portfolio valuation immediately.
### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

Stock splits multiply shares by the split factor while dividing price proportionally; bonus shares add proportional free shares:

Split Shares = Initial * (New / Old)  |  Bonus Shares = Initial * (BonusNew / BonusOld)  |  Adjusted Price = Total Value / Total Shares

Worked Example: Holding 100 shares priced at $1,000 each ($100,000 portfolio) undergoing a 2-for-1 stock split combined with a 1-for-1 bonus issue results in **400 total shares** at an adjusted price of **$250.00**, maintaining a total portfolio value of **$100,000**.
