How Zakat, Nisab Thresholds (Gold & Silver), and 2.5% Calculations Work

### Why Zakat Calculators Are Essential for Muslims Worldwide

**Zakat** is one of the Five Pillars of Islam, requiring every qualifying Muslim to donate a fixed percentage (typically **2.5%**) of their accumulated wealth and savings annually to those in need. To be obligated to pay Zakat, an individual's total wealth must equal or exceed the **Nisab threshold**—the minimum exemption limit established during the time of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) based on the value of **87.48 grams of gold** or **612.36 grams of silver**. A Zakat calculator computes total asset values across cash, bank deposits, gold, silver, and business inventory, comparing them against live Nisab limits to determine precise Zakat obligations. Our browser-based tool calculates Zakat locally in client memory without server uploads.

### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Zakat equals 2.5% of total eligible zakatable assets whenever total wealth exceeds the silver Nisab threshold:

Silver Nisab = 612.36g * Silver Rate  |  Total Assets = Gold + Silver + Cash + Business  |  Zakat Due = Total Assets * 0.025
