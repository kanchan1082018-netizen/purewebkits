Complete User Guide to Vedic Kundli Milan, Ashtakoot Gun Milan (36 Points) & Dosha Checks

How Ashtakoot Gun Milan Evaluates Compatibility Out of 36 Points

In traditional Vedic astrology (Jyotish), Kundli Milan (also called Gun Milan or Horoscope Matching) evaluates marriage compatibility between prospective partners using the Ashtakoot ("eight-fold") system, which awards up to 36 total points (Gunas) based on each partner's Janma Nakshatra (lunar mansion, out of 27) and Chandra Rashi (Moon sign, out of 12). Each of the eight Kootas carries an ascending weight from 1 to 8 points: Varna (1), Vashya (2), Tara (3), Yoni (4), Graha Maitri (5), Gana (6), Bhakoot (7), and Nadi (8). Traditionally, a total score of 18 or higher out of 36 is considered acceptable, 24 to 31 is considered a strong match, and 32 to 36 is considered exceptional. A browser-based Kundli Milan Calculator computes all eight Koota scores, Rashi/Nakshatra attributes, and Nadi/Bhakoot Dosha alerts 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

The 8 Ashtakoot Gun Milan Components (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 = 36)

Summing all eight Koota scores produces the total Ashtakoot compatibility score:

Total Gun Milan (Max 36) = Varna (1) + Vashya (2) + Tara (3) + Yoni (4) + Graha Maitri (5) + Gana (6) + Bhakoot (7) + Nadi (8)

Helpful Tips When Interpreting Gun Milan & Dosha Results

  • Understand Nadi Dosha (8 Points) & Bhakoot Dosha (7 Points): When both partners share the same Nadi (Adi, Madhya, or Antya), Nadi scores 0/8; certain traditional exceptions (such as same Rashi with different Nakshatras or same Nakshatra with different Rashis) are evaluated by a qualified astrologer alongside full birth charts.
  • Check Mangal Dosha (Kuja Dosha) Separately With Birth Place: Full Lagna/Mars chart matching depends on exact birth coordinates and ascendant degree—consult a full Panchang/Kundli chart for Manglik evaluation.
  • Cultural & Educational Tool: This calculator implements classical Ashtakoot lookup tables for informational and educational reference.
