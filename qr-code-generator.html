Minimum Print Size & Scanning Distance Formula + Worked Example

Print engineers determine the minimum physical width of a printed QR code using the industry-standard 10:1 Scan-Distance Ratio:

Minimum Printed QR Width = Scanning Distance / 10

Worked Example: Suppose you are printing a storefront window poster or event standee that customers will scan from 1.5 meters (150 cm / ~59 inches) away.

Minimum QR Width = 150 cm / 10 = 15 cm (approximately 6 inches × 6 inches).

Downloading the Vector .SVG file and scaling it to 15 × 15 cm with Level Q error correction guarantees instant camera recognition even through glass reflections.
