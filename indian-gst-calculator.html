How Indian GST Calculation, CGST/SGST Split & Reverse GST Work

Understanding Intra-State (CGST + SGST) vs. Inter-State (IGST)

India's Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a unified destination-based indirect tax governed by the GST Council across five primary slabs: 3% (gold, silver, and precious jewellery), 5% (essential food items, economy air travel, and transport), 12% (processed foods, computers, and business-class flights), 18% (IT/software services, restaurant dining, electronics, and financial services), and 28% (automobiles, cement, and luxury goods). When a supplier and buyer are located within the same state or union territory, the applicable GST rate is split equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST/UTGST (State GST)—for example, an 18% slab becomes 9% CGST + 9% SGST. When goods or services cross state borders, the entire 18% is billed as a single IGST (Integrated GST).

How to Calculate Indian GST in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Select Exclusive or Inclusive Mode: Choose + Add GST (Exclusive) to add tax onto a base price, or − Remove GST (Inclusive) to extract the base price and GST from an MRP or gross bill.
  2. Choose Same-State or Inter-State Supply: Toggle between Same State (CGST + SGST) for a 50%-50% tax split and Inter-State (IGST) for interstate B2B/B2C invoices.
  3. Pick GST Slab & Copy Invoice Values: Click 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% and copy the exact Net, CGST/SGST, and Total figures for your GST invoice or GSTR-1 filing.

GST Exclusive & GST Inclusive Formulas + Worked Example

Under Indian GST invoicing rules, reverse-calculating the taxable base price from a GST-inclusive price divides the gross amount by 1 + (GST Rate / 100):

Add GST: Total = Base × (1 + Rate/100)  |  Remove GST: Base = Total / (1 + Rate/100)

Worked Example (GST Inclusive Extraction at 18%): Suppose a digital marketing agency in Patna, Bihar issues a GST-inclusive invoice of ₹23,600 to a client in Bihar under the 18% GST slab.

Taxable Net Base Price = ₹23,600 / 1.18 = ₹20,000.00.

Total GST Amount = ₹23,600 − ₹20,000 = ₹3,600.00, split equally into ₹1,800.00 CGST (9%) and ₹1,800.00 SGST (9%).
