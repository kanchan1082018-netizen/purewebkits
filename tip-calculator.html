Complete User Guide to Calculating Tips, Splitting Group Bills, and Rounding Totals

Why Tip Calculators Make Dining and Group Expenses Stress-Free

Splitting a restaurant check, café order, delivery bill, or group outing at the end of a meal can get awkward when you are trying to compute a percentage in your head and divide it evenly across several people. A browser-based Tip Calculator computes the tip amount, total bill, and per-person share immediately—complete with quick percentage presets, optional rounding up to a whole unit, and support for multiple currency symbols. Because everything runs 100% client-side inside your browser using JavaScript, your bill amounts and expense notes stay strictly on your device without server uploads.

How to Calculate Tips and Split a Bill in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Enter Bill Amount & Currency: Choose your preferred currency symbol and type the bill amount into Bill Amount, or click Load Sample Bill to test.
  2. Pick Tip Percentage & Group Size: Click a quick percentage preset (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, or 25%) or enter a custom tip percentage, and set how many people are sharing the bill.
  3. Optionally Round Up & Copy Summary: Check Round total bill up to nearest whole currency unit if you prefer clean whole numbers, then click Copy Split Summary or Download .txt.

Tip & Group Split Formulas

Given bill amount B, tip percentage P, and group size N ≥ 1:

Tip Amount = B × (P / 100)  |  Total Bill = B + Tip Amount
Per Person Total = (Optional Rounded Total) / N

Helpful Tips for Group Bill Splitting

  • Check Whether Service Charge Is Already Included: Before adding a tip, check your bill receipt to see if a service charge or gratuity is already added.
  • Use Whole-Unit Rounding for Cash or Quick Transfers: Enabling total rounding helps avoid awkward coins or fractional cents when settling up with friends.
  • Adjust Split Count for Solo vs. Group Meals: Set Split Between (People) to 1 for an individual bill or increase it to match the number of participants.

Worked Example: On a $120.00 bill with an 18% tip, the tip is $21.60 and the total is $141.60. Split evenly among 3 people, each person pays $47.20 ($40.00 base + $7.20 tip).
