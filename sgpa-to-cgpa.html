Complete User Guide to Converting SGPA to CGPA & University Percentage Formulas

Why Credit-Weighted CGPA Differs From a Simple Average of SGPAs

Under the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) used across universities in India (such as AICTE, UGC, VTU, AKTU, SPPU, Mumbai University, and Anna University) and international credit-hour institutions, each semester yields a Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) calculated from that term's course credits and grade points. When combining multiple semesters into a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA), each semester's SGPA is weighted by the total credit load registered in that semester: CGPA = Σ(SGPAi × Creditsi) / ΣCreditsi. If every semester carries the exact same credit count (for example, 22 credits per term), the credit-weighted CGPA equals the simple arithmetic mean of your SGPAs; however, when project semesters, internships, or lab-heavy terms carry different credit totals, using Credit-Weighted CGPA (Exact) ensures your calculated CGPA matches your official marksheet to the second decimal place. SGPA to CGPA & CGPA to Percentage Equations

Using semester SGPAs Si and semester credit loads Ci:

Credit-Weighted CGPA = Σ(Si × Ci) / ΣCi  |  Equal-Credit CGPA = (S1 + S2 + ... + Sn) / n
CBSE / AICTE % = CGPA × 9.5  |  VTU / SPPU / Mumbai % = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10

Helpful Tips for Transcript & Placement Applications

  • Verify Your University's Official Percentage Conversion Rule: While CGPA × 9.5 is standard for CBSE and many AICTE institutions, universities such as VTU, SPPU (Pune), and Mumbai University publish specific conversion formulas printed on the back of the consolidated marksheet.
  • Use Total Earned Credits (Excluding Non-Credit Audit Courses): Enter only credit-bearing GPA credits for each semester as listed on your semester grade card.
  • Track Semester Trend Along With Cumulative CGPA: Displaying highest and lowest semester SGPAs helps you verify upward academic momentum across terms.
