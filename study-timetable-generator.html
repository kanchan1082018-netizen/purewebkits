How Spaced Repetition, Time-Blocking & Exam Revision Planning Work

### Why Study Timetable & Revision Planners Are Essential for Students

Preparing for major examinations, board exams, or college semesters without a structured schedule often leads to last-minute cramming, burnout, and poor retention. Effective study planning relies on **time-blocking** and **spaced repetition**—distributing study sessions across multiple days to transfer knowledge into long-term memory. A study timetable & exam revision planner automatically distributes your subjects and daily study hours into a structured preparation roadmap. Our browser-based tool builds personalized schedules locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Create Your Study Timetable in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Subjects: Type or paste your course subjects into the input box, with one subject per line.
  2. Set Daily Hours & Date: Specify how many hours you can study daily and select your target exam date.
  3. Generate & Follow: Click Generate Timetable to receive your customized daily revision rotation and study blocks.
### Scheduling Logic + Worked Example

Study timetables rotate through subject lists based on available daily hours and days remaining until exam deadlines:

Subject Daily Allocation = Total Daily Hours / Number of Subjects  |  Rotation = Subject List Cyclic Distribution

Worked Example: Entering 5 subjects with 4 hours of daily study time allocates ~48 minutes per subject daily, rotating systematically through theoretical study and problem-solving practice.
