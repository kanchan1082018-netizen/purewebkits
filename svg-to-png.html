How Vector SVG-to-PNG Rasterization & High-DPI Scaling Work

Why Developers & Designers Convert Scalable SVG Vectors to PNG or JPG

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) stores icons, brand logos, and illustrations as XML mathematical paths rather than fixed pixel grids—allowing them to scale infinitely without blur. However, social media platforms (Open Graph social cards, YouTube thumbnails, WhatsApp, Instagram), email newsletters (Gmail and Outlook block inline SVGs for security), and mobile app stores require standard raster .png or .jpg files. Rendering an SVG onto an HTML5 Canvas at a 2x, 3x, or 4x resolution multiplier lets you export razor-sharp, anti-aliased Retina PNGs with full alpha transparency—or solid-background JPGs—directly inside your browser.

How to Convert SVG to PNG or JPG in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Upload .SVG or Paste Code: Drop any .svg file into the upload zone or paste raw markup into the editor.
  2. Select Format & Resolution Scale: Pick PNG (.png) for transparent icons or JPG (.jpg) for social cards, and select 1x, 2x, 3x, or 4x scaling.
  3. Inspect Preview & Download: Verify transparency on the checkerboard preview and click Download PNG/JPG Image.

Vector-to-Raster Pixel Scaling Formula + Worked Example

Exported pixel dimensions equal the SVG's intrinsic width and height (or viewBox dimensions) multiplied by your chosen DPI scale factor:

Output Pixel Width = SVG Base Width × Scale Multiplier (1x–4x)

Worked Example: Suppose your brand logo SVG has a viewBox="0 0 256 256" (256 × 256 px base size).

Selecting 4x (4K Print / App Icon) multiplies both dimensions by 4: 256 × 4 = 1,024 px.

The tool exports a crystal-clear 1024 × 1024 px PNG—meeting Apple App Store and Google Play high-resolution icon requirements with zero pixelation.
