Complete User Guide to Affiliate Commissions, Earnings Per Click (EPC) & Traffic Goal Planning

How Conversion Rate, Commission %, and Earnings Per Click (EPC) Drive Affiliate Revenue

Whether you monetize a content website, YouTube channel, newsletter, or review hub through e-commerce affiliate networks or SaaS partner programs, your monthly payout is governed by four levers: qualified affiliate clicks, merchant conversion rate (CVR %), average order value (AOV), and your commission percentage (adjusted for typical refund or cancellation rates). Combining those ratios into a single north-star metric—Earnings Per Click (EPC), calculated as Net Commission / Total Clicks—allows creators to compare two very different partner offers on equal footing: for instance, a high-ticket SaaS program converting at 1.5% with a 30% commission can easily outperform a physical product converting at 5% with a 3% commission. Affiliate Payout, Net EPC & Reverse Traffic Formulas

Let Clicks be monthly outbound link clicks, CVR = Conversion% / 100, AOV be average order value, Comm = Commission% / 100, and Ref = Refund% / 100:

Net Commission / Sale = AOV × Comm × (1 − Ref)  |  Net EPC = CVR × Net Commission / Sale
Monthly Commission = Clicks × Net EPC  |  Required Clicks for Goal = Target Income / Net EPC

Helpful Tips for Optimizing Affiliate Conversions & EPC

  • Account for Refund Windows & Cookie Attribution: Many programs hold commissions for 30 to 60 days to deduct returns or canceled trials—including a realistic 3%–10% refund buffer keeps forecasts grounded.
  • Compare EPC Across Programs: Track outbound clicks per page and compare EPC rather than looking only at headline commission percentages.
  • Disclose Affiliate Relationships Clearly: Always include clear affiliate disclosures in compliance with platform rules and applicable advertising guidelines.

Worked Example: Sending 10,000 monthly clicks at a 2.5% conversion rate generates 250 orders. At an $80 AOV and 15% commission ($12.00 gross per sale), deducting a 5% refund rate yields $11.40 net per sale, $0.29 net EPC, and $2,850.00/month ($34,200.00/year).
