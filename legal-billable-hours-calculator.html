How Legal Billable Hours, 0.1-Hour Increments (6-Minute Slabs) & Fee Calculations Work

### Why Legal Billable Hours Calculators Are Essential for Attorneys & Clients

In the legal profession, time is the primary commodity. Law firms and independent attorneys traditionally bill clients using fractional hour increments—most commonly the **0.1-hour (6-minute) billing slab**. Under this standard system, each hour is divided into ten equal segments of six minutes each. Any task performed must be rounded up to the nearest 0.1-hour increment. For instance, a 14-minute phone call is billed as 0.3 hours (18 minutes). A legal billable hours & fee calculator automatically converts minutes into exact 0.1-hour billing increments and multiplies them by the attorney's hourly rate. Our browser-based tool calculates billing disclosures locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Billable Hours in 3 Easy Steps

  2. Enter Minutes Worked: Type the exact duration spent on client legal work in minutes.

### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Billable hour rounding divides worked minutes by 60, rounds up to the nearest tenth of an hour, and multiplies by the hourly rate:

Decimal Hours = Minutes / 60  |  Rounded Hours = Math.ceil(Decimal Hours * 10) / 10  |  Total Fee = Rounded Hours * Hourly Rate

Worked Example: Working 45 minutes at an hourly rate of $350 yields 0.75 exact decimal hours, which rounds up to **0.8 billable hours** under the 0.1-hour slab, resulting in a total legal fee of **$280.00**.
