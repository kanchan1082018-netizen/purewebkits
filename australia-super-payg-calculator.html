How Australian Superannuation Guarantee, PAYG Tax Brackets & Net Salary Work

### Why Australian Super & PAYG Calculators Are Essential for Employees and Employers

Working in Australia involves understanding two major financial components mandated by the Australian Taxation Office (**ATO**): **Superannuation Guarantee (Super)** and **PAYG (Pay-As-You-Earn)** income tax. Employers must pay a statutory percentage into an employee's chosen super fund (scaling up to 12% in 2025-2026), separate from or inclusive of the base salary package. Simultaneously, progressive income tax brackets and the Medicare levy determine the net take-home pay. A Australian superannuation & PAYG tax calculator computes employer super contributions and net salary instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates tax liabilities locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Australian Super & PAYG in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Gross Salary: Type your annual or monthly base salary into the input field.
  2. Select Super Rate: Choose the applicable Superannuation Guarantee percentage (11.5% or 12.0%).
  3. Review Net Pay & Super: Instantly inspect your employer superannuation contribution, ATO PAYG tax deduction, and net take-home salary.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Super contributions multiply base salary by the super rate, while PAYG tax applies progressive tax brackets minus the low-income tax offset and Medicare levy:

Super Contribution = Salary * Super Rate  |  Net Salary = Base Salary - PAYG Tax Liability

Worked Example: Earning an annual gross salary of A$95,000 with a 11.5% super guarantee (exclusive) generates an employer super contribution of **A$ 10,925**, an ATO PAYG tax liability of **A$ 22,232**, and a **Net Take-Home Pay of A$ 72,768**.
