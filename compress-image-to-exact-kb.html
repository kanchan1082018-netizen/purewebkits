How to Use the Exact KB Image Compressor

Online applications for passports, government exams, and visas enforce strict file size caps. This tool uses an intelligent client-side binary search algorithm inside your browser's HTML5 Canvas to shrink your photo just below your exact KB limit while preserving sharp facial details.

  1. Upload Your Photo: Drag and drop any JPG, PNG, or WebP file into the upload box above, or click Choose Image File.
  2. Set Your Target KB: Click a quick preset (20 KB, 50 KB, 100 KB, or 200 KB) or type any custom KB target.
  3. Choose Output Format: Select .JPEG for photographs and official portals (best for hitting small KB targets), .PNG for lossless graphics or transparent backgrounds, or .WEBP for lightweight web images.
  4. Inspect & Download: Compare the side-by-side preview and savings, then click Download Compressed Image.

Client-Side Compression: JPEG vs. PNG

JPEG uses lossy compression, allowing our binary search engine to fine-tune quality from 98% down to 5% (and proportionally adjust dimensions only if necessary) to hit tiny targets like 20 KB without making text or faces unreadable. PNG is lossless and preserves transparency; because PNG ignores quality sliders, hitting a strict KB limit in PNG automatically scales image dimensions until the file fits under your target.

Compression Ratio = Original Size (KB) / Compressed Size (KB)  |  Space Saved (%) = (1 − Compressed / Original) × 100
Worked Example (Exam Form Upload): Suppose your smartphone captures a 1.8 MB (1,843.2 KB) portrait photo, and an exam portal requires a JPEG under 50 KB. Entering 50 KB and selecting .JPEG runs a binary search across quality levels, delivering a crisp 48.5 KB file. Using the formula: Compression Ratio = 1843.2 / 48.5 = 38.0×, saving 97.4% of the original file size in under one second.
