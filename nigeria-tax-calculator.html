How Nigerian PAYE Income Tax, Pension Act 2014 & CRA Relief Work

### Why Nigerian Tax & Pension Calculators Are Essential for Employees and Employers

Employment income in Nigeria is subject to Pay-As-You-Earn (**PAYE**) income tax, administered by state internal revenue services under federal Personal Income Tax Act (PITA) guidelines. Taxable income calculations incorporate mandatory statutory deductions, notably the **8% employee pension contribution** (under the Pension Reform Act 2014) and the **Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA)**, which shields a higher percentage of lower earnings from heavy taxation. A Nigeria PAYE tax & pension calculator computes employee tax liabilities and net take-home pay instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates statutory deductions locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Nigerian PAYE Tax in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Gross Salary: Type your total annual or monthly gross earnings before deductions.
  2. Select Salary Period: Choose whether your salary input is calculated annually or monthly.
  3. Verify Pension Option: Include or exclude the mandatory 8% employee pension contribution to inspect your net take-home pay immediately.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Taxable income subtracts pension contributions and Consolidated Relief Allowance (CRA) from gross salary before applying progressive tax brackets:

CRA = Higher of (₦200k or 1% of Gross) + 20% of Gross  |  Taxable Income = Gross - Pension - CRA

Worked Example: Earning an annual gross salary of ₦6,000,000 with mandatory 8% pension (₦480,000) results in an annual PAYE tax liability of **₦702,500** and a **Net Take-Home Pay of ₦4,817,500**.
