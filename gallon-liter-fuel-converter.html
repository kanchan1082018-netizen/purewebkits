Comprehensive Guide to US Gallons, Liters, and Fuel Economy Conversions (MPG to L/100km)

### Why Fuel Volume and Economy Converters Are Essential for International Travel and Vehicles

Converting fuel volume and vehicle fuel economy measurements between US standards and metric standards is a frequent necessity when importing vehicles, traveling internationally, or comparing fuel efficiency specifications across global automobile markets. While the United States measures fuel volume in **US Gallons** and fuel economy in **Miles Per Gallon (MPG)**, most of the world utilizes the metric system, measuring volume in **Liters** and fuel consumption in **Liters per 100 kilometers (L/100km)** or **Kilometers per Liter (km/L)**. Additionally, understanding the inverse relationship between MPG and L/100km can be confusing: while higher MPG indicates greater fuel efficiency (more distance per gallon), lower L/100km indicates greater efficiency (fewer liters consumed per 100 kilometers driven). A professional **US Gallon to Liter & MPG to L/100km Converter** enables drivers, automotive enthusiasts, and travelers to input fuel volumes or US MPG ratings to compute equivalent metric volumes and consumption rates instantly. Our browser-based utility calculates fuel conversions locally in client memory without external server uploads, guaranteeing absolute data privacy and lightning-fast performance.

### Fuel Conversion Formulas + Worked Example

US volume and fuel economy ratings are converted to metric equivalents using precise international conversion factors:

$\text{Liters} = \text{US Gallons} \times 3.785411784$  |  $\text{L/100km} = \frac{235.214583}{\text{US MPG}}$
