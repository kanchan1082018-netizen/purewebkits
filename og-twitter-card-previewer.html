How Open Graph (OG) Tags and Twitter Cards Work and Why They Are Crucial for Social Media SEO

### Why Open Graph & Twitter Card Previewers Are Essential for Content Creators and Marketers

When you share a link on Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, WhatsApp, or Slack, the platform scans your target webpage for **Open Graph (OG)** meta tags and Twitter Card markup to generate a rich visual preview card. Without properly structured OG tags, social networks pull random text and broken images, resulting in low click-through rates and poor brand presentation. An **Open Graph & Twitter Card Previewer** allows webmasters and digital marketers to test and visualize their title, description, and preview image before publishing links live. Our browser-based utility renders mock social cards locally in client memory without external server uploads, guaranteeing absolute privacy and instant feedback.

### How to Preview Social Cards in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Meta Details: Type or paste your proposed page title, description, canonical URL, and preview banner image link.
  2. Select Platform View: Switch between standard Facebook/LinkedIn landscape cards and Twitter/X card layouts.
  3. Optimize & Refine: Adjust text lengths and image dimensions immediately to ensure high-converting social visibility.
