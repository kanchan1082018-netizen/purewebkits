How 2D Matrix Image Rotation, Mirroring & Bounding-Box Expansion Work

Fixing Sideways Phone Scans, Reversed Selfies & Tilted Horizon Lines

Smartphones and document scanners frequently embed orientation flags inside EXIF metadata rather than rotating the underlying pixel grid—causing scanned IDs, certificates, or portrait photos to appear sideways or upside down when uploaded to web portals. Similarly, front-facing selfie cameras capture horizontally mirrored images where text on clothing or signs appears backward. Our browser-based Rotate & Flip Image tool applies true 2D affine matrix transformations directly onto an HTML5 Canvas—permanently baking your 90°, 180°, fine-angle straighten, or horizontal/vertical mirror adjustments into a clean, universally compatible JPG, PNG, or WebP file.

  2. Rotate or Mirror Instantly: Click Rotate Left 90°, Rotate Right 90°, 180°, Flip Horizontal, or Flip Vertical—or drag the Fine Straighten slider to level tilted horizons.
  3. Choose Format & Download: Select JPG, PNG, or WebP and click Download Transformed Image.

2D Rotation Bounding-Box Formula & Worked Example

When an image of width W and height H is rotated by an arbitrary angle θ, the canvas expands to fit all four rotated corners without clipping edges:

Wnew = |W × cos(θ)| + |H × sin(θ)|  |  Hnew = |W × sin(θ)| + |H × cos(θ)|

Worked Example: Suppose you upload a landscape scanned document measuring 1600 × 1200 px and rotate it 90° clockwise.

Since cos(90°) = 0 and sin(90°) = 1: Wnew = |1600 × 0| + |1200 × 1| = 1,200 px and Hnew = |1600 × 1| + |1200 × 0| = 1,600 px.

The exported file is a crisp portrait 1200 × 1600 px image with zero cropped corners.
