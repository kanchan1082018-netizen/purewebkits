How Brazilian CPF and CNPJ Checksum Algorithms and Format Validation Work

### Why Brazilian Tax ID Validators & Generators Are Essential for Software Developers

Building web applications, e-commerce checkouts, or financial software for the Brazilian market requires rigorous verification of national tax numbers. **CPF (Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas)** is an 11-digit number assigned to individuals for tax and identification purposes, while **CNPJ (Cadastro Nacional da Pessoa Jurídica)** is a 14-digit number assigned to companies and corporate entities. Both documents rely on strict mathematical **Modulus 11 checksum algorithms** to prevent fraudulent or mistyped entries. A Brazil CPF & CNPJ validator and test generator verifies checksum digits and creates valid test numbers instantly. Our browser-based tool processes validations locally in client memory without server uploads.

### Checksum Formula + Worked Example

Brazilian tax numbers use weighted multipliers descending from 9 or 10 down to 2, calculating remainders via Modulus 11:

Remainder = Sum(Digit * Weight) % 11  |  Check Digit = Remainder < 2 ? 0 : 11 - Remainder

Worked Example: Validating a 11-digit CPF string multiplies the first 9 digits by descending weights (10 down to 2), summing products to calculate the 10th and 11th verification check digits.
