How BMR, TDEE & Daily Calorie Calculations Work

### Why Calculating BMR and TDEE Is Essential for Fitness Goals

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) represents the minimum number of calories your body needs to maintain basic life-sustaining functions (like breathing and circulation) at complete rest. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) accounts for your BMR plus physical activity and exercise throughout the day. Knowing your TDEE is critical whether you want to lose weight, maintain your physique, or build muscle. Our browser-based Daily Calorie & TDEE Calculator computes energy requirements instantly in client memory without server uploads.

### Mifflin-St Jeor Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Calorie expenditure calculations use the scientifically validated Mifflin-St Jeor equation:

BMR (Male) = (10 * weight) + (6.25 * height) - (5 * age) + 5  |  TDEE = BMR * Activity Multiplier

Worked Example: For a 25-year-old male weighing 70 kg, 175 cm tall, with moderate activity (1.55 multiplier), BMR is 1,662 kcal, resulting in a TDEE of 2,576 calories/day.
