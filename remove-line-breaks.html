Complete User Guide to Removing Line Breaks, Fixing Wrapped Paragraphs, and Formatting Lists

Why Removing Line Breaks Matters When Copying Text Across Tools

Copying paragraphs from PDF documents, narrow email panes, terminal logs, or scanned articles frequently introduces hard line breaks (\n or \r\n) in the middle of sentences. When you paste that text into a CMS editor, translation form, search query, or chat message, those awkward line wraps stay stuck where they do not belong—breaking sentences into choppy fragments or turning a list into an unformatted block. A browser-based Line Break Remover strips line breaks across your text and joins lines cleanly using a space, comma, custom separator, or direct concatenation, all locally inside your browser without uploading your text to any external server.

How to Remove Line Breaks in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Paste Your Multi-Line Text: Insert text containing unwanted newlines into the Multi-Line Text Input box, or click Load Sample Text to test immediately.
  2. Choose How Lines Are Joined: Select Single Space for normal prose paragraphs, Comma + Space for turning lines into a comma-separated list, or Nothing when joining character fragments or tokens.
  3. Copy or Download the Result: Enable Collapse extra spaces if needed to tidy double spaces, then click Copy Cleaned Text or export a .txt file.

Newline Normalization & Join Formula

Line-break cleanup normalizes Windows (\r\n) and classic Mac (\r) newlines to standard Unix line feeds (\n), splits lines, and joins them with your chosen delimiter:

Normalized Text = text.replace(/\r\n?/g, "\n") → join(lines, delimiter) → optional collapseSpaces(" +" → " ")

Tips for Clean Text Formatting

  • Use Single Space for Paragraphs: When fixing PDF-copied sentences, a single space prevents words at the end of one line and start of the next from sticking together.
  • Use Comma + Space for Inline Lists: Converting a vertical list of tags, emails, or terms into item1, item2, item3 takes one click with the comma separator.
  • Combine With Other Text Utilities: After joining lines, you can trim leading/trailing spaces, deduplicate lines, or check word counts using companion PureWebKits utilities.
