Complete User Guide to Inviting Card Design, Custom Sizing & A4 Multi-Card Printing

How Custom Invitation Dimensions, Border Styling & A4 Multi-Up Layouts Work

Whether you are preparing wedding invitations, birthday party cards, engagement announcements, baby shower invites, housewarming (Griha Pravesh) cards, festive greetings, or school/community event invitations, having complete control over card dimensions, typography, and print layout saves time and paper. Standard greeting and invitation cards commonly use 5 × 7 inches (1500 × 2100 px at 300 DPI) or 4 × 6 inches (1200 × 1800 px at 300 DPI), while custom width and height inputs let you match any envelope size you already own. You can choose from 25 ready-to-edit templates or upload your own custom card artwork/background image and overlay editable text with Bold, Italic, adjustable font scaling (A- / A+), and customizable border colors and styles—without any unwanted branding or logos on your card. When printing at home or at a local print shop, packing 2, 3, or 4 identical cards onto a single A4 sheet (8.27 × 11.69 inches) with dashed cut guides maximizes paper efficiency. A browser-based Inviting Card Maker processes everything 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

A4 Multi-Up Sheet Packing & 300 DPI Resolution Formula

At 300 DPI, an international A4 sheet (210 × 297 mm = 8.27 × 11.69 in) measures 2480 × 3508 pixels, allowing clean multi-card packing:

Card Pixels = Width_in × 300 × Height_in × 300  |  A4 Sheet @ 300 DPI = 2480 × 3508 px
A4 Multi-Up Options: 1-Up (Centered), 2-Up (Side-by-Side / Stacked), 3-Up (3 Rows), 4-Up (2 × 2 Grid)

Helpful Tips for Crisp Invitation Printing

  • Use 250–300 GSM Cardstock for Invitations: Standard office paper is 70–80 GSM; printing your A4 multi-card sheet on heavier matte, ivory, or glossy cardstock gives invitations a sturdy, premium feel.
  • Set Print Scale to "100% / Actual Size" in Your Print Dialog: Disable "Fit to Printable Area" if you want exact ruler-accurate card dimensions when trimming along the dashed cut guides.
  • Check Text Contrast When Uploading Custom Card Images: If you upload a colorful custom card photo, adjust Text Color and Heading / Accent Color so names, dates, and venue lines remain easy to read.

Worked Example: Designing a 4 × 6 inch birthday or wedding card and choosing 4 Cards per A4 Page (2 × 2 Grid) packs four crisp cards with dashed trim lines onto one A4 sheet so you can print and cut four invitations per page.
