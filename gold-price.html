Complete User Guide to Gold Karat Purity, Weight Conversions, Making Charges & Tax

How Karat Purity, Making Charges, and Tax Shape Final Gold Pricing

Gold bullion and jewelry prices depend on three main building blocks: the underlying market rate for pure 24-karat (24K) gold, the karat purity of the item (such as 22K jewelry at 22/24 ≈ 91.67% fine gold, 18K at 75.00%, or 14K at 58.33%), and retail additions including making charges (craftsmanship, design, and wastage) plus applicable tax / GST / VAT. Because gold rates fluctuate and weight units vary by region—including grams (g), tolas (11.6638 g), and troy ounces (31.1034768 g)—having a transparent breakdown helps buyers verify quotes quickly. Gold Purity, Making Charges & Tax Formula

Let R_24 be the 24K rate per gram, W_g be weight in grams, F = Karat / 24 be purity fineness, M% be making charge percentage, and T% be tax percentage:

Purity Rate / g = R_24 × (Karat / 24)  |  Base Gold Value = Purity Rate / g × W_g
Making Charges = Base Gold Value × (M% / 100)  |  Tax = (Base + Making) × (T% / 100)

Helpful Tips When Buying Gold or Jewelry

  • Check Hallmarking & Purity Marks: Look for official hallmarking/fineness stamps and ask for an itemized invoice showing gold weight, karat rate, making charges, and tax separately.
  • Verify How Making Charges Are Applied: Jewelers may quote making charges as a percentage of gold value or a flat per-gram charge—enter the equivalent percentage in the input field.
  • Compare Buyback & Exchange Terms: Making charges and taxes paid at retail purchase are typically not recovered on resale or exchange.

Worked Example: At a 24K rate of ₹7,500/g, 10 g of 22K gold (22/24 purity) has a 22K rate of ₹6,875.00/g and a base gold value of ₹68,750.00. Adding 10% making charges (₹6,875.00) gives a ₹75,625.00 subtotal, and 3% tax on that subtotal (₹2,268.75) yields a final total of ₹77,893.75.
