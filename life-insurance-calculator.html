Complete User Guide to Life Insurance Coverage Planning & the DIME Needs-Analysis Method

Why Needs-Based Coverage Beats Rough Rules of Thumb

Many buyers hear a simple rule of thumb like "buy 10 times your annual salary" when shopping for term life insurance, yet two households earning the exact same income can have vastly different protection needs depending on mortgage balances, consumer loans, children's education goals, existing employer group coverage, and liquid savings. Financial planners commonly organize these obligations using the DIME framework—Debt and final expenses, Income replacement over your dependents' horizon, Mortgage payoff, and Education funding—and then subtract existing life insurance policies and accessible savings to isolate the true net coverage gap. A browser-based Life Insurance Calculator runs this entire needs analysis 100% locally in your browser using JavaScript—with zero server uploads.

DIME Life Insurance Needs Formula

Summing household obligations and subtracting existing resources produces your net recommended policy face amount:

Gross Need = (Annual Income × Replacement Years) + Debts & Mortgage + Education & Final Costs
Recommended Additional Coverage = max(0, Gross Need − Existing Cover & Liquid Savings)

Helpful Tips When Choosing a Life Insurance Policy

  • Match Policy Term Length to Your Longest Obligation: For instance, if you have a 20-year mortgage or young children, a 20-year or 25-year level term policy aligns coverage with those peak dependency years.
  • Do Not Rely Solely on Employer Group Coverage: Workplace life insurance is often limited to 1x–2x salary and typically ends if you change jobs.
  • Educational Planning Tool Only: This calculator provides mathematical needs estimation only and is not licensed insurance, tax, or financial advice.

Worked Example: Replacing $85,000/year for 10 years ($850,000), paying off $220,000 in mortgage/debts, and funding $80,000 in education/final expenses creates a $1,150,000 gross need. Subtracting $150,000 in existing coverage and savings leaves $1,000,000 (11.76x annual income) in recommended additional coverage.
