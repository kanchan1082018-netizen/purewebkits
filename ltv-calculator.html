Complete User Guide to Customer Lifetime Value (LTV), Churn Lifespan, Gross Margin & LTV:CAC Ratio

Why Gross-Margin-Adjusted LTV and LTV:CAC Govern Sustainable Growth

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV or CLV) estimates the total net economic contribution an average customer generates over their entire relationship with your business. In subscription and SaaS companies, average customer lifespan in months is inversely proportional to monthly logo/revenue churn (Avg. Lifespan = 1 / Monthly Churn Rate), so multiplying Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) by lifespan and Gross Margin (%) reveals how much gross profit each cohort generates before sales and marketing overhead. In repeat e-commerce or retail businesses, LTV is driven by Average Order Value (AOV), purchase frequency per year, retention lifespan in years, and gross margin. Comparing margin-adjusted LTV against Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)—where an LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 or higher with a CAC payback period under 12 months is widely cited as a healthy benchmark—helps founders decide how aggressively to scale acquisition. SaaS & E-Commerce LTV Formulas

Using gross margin fraction GM = Gross Margin% / 100 keeps LTV aligned with actual contribution dollars:

SaaS: Avg. Lifespan (mo) = 1 / (Churn% / 100)  |  Net LTV = ARPU × Lifespan (mo) × GM
E-Commerce: Net LTV = AOV × Orders/Yr × Lifespan (yrs) × GM  |  LTV:CAC = Net LTV / CAC

Helpful Tips for Accurate LTV Modeling

  • Always Multiply by Gross Margin (%): Comparing unadjusted revenue LTV against CAC overstates unit economics, especially in e-commerce where COGS and fulfillment consume 35%–65% of revenue.
  • Watch Small Churn Improvements: Cutting monthly churn from 5% to 2.5% doubles average customer lifespan from 20 months to 40 months.
  • Include Full Acquisition Costs in CAC: Combine ad spend, agency/creative costs, and sales commissions when entering CAC.

Worked Example: A SaaS product with $60 monthly ARPU, 4% monthly churn (1 / 0.04 = 25.0 months average lifespan), and 80% gross margin generates $1,500.00 in gross revenue LTV and $1,200.00 in margin-adjusted LTV. At a $300 CAC, LTV:CAC = 4.00 : 1 with a 6.25-month gross-margin payback period.
