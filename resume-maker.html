Complete Guide to Using the Resume Maker, Custom Tables & Photo Controls

How to Build & Customize Your Resume on the Side Preview

This Resume Maker keeps your editing controls on one side and a live A4 (210mm x 297mm) sheet preview right alongside it so every change you make appears immediately. You can edit your personal details, summary, experience, education, and skills, add custom structured tables for skills matrices or academic marks, and position or resize your photo with full control. Because everything runs locally in your browser, your photo and personal details stay on your device with zero server uploads.

How to Use This Tool in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Upload or Adjust Your Photo (Optional): Click Upload Photo to add a photo, use Size to make it smaller or larger, use Left / Right (X) and Up / Down (Y) sliders to position it, or choose No Photo / click Remove Photo.
  2. Edit Details & Create Custom Tables: Fill or edit your name, role, contact info, summary, experience, education, skills, and click + Create Table to add rows, columns, and editable table cells.
  3. Print / Save as PDF or Download: Click Print / Save as PDF for an exact A4 sheet, or click Download PNG / Download .txt.
A4 Sheet Size = 210mm x 297mm | Photo Position = (X offset, Y offset) + Custom Width/Height (px)

Example: Click + Create Table to add a 3-column skills/marks table, type your rows and cells, adjust your photo position slightly right and down, and click Print / Save as PDF.
