How Section 80D Tax Deductions, Health Insurance Limits & Savings Work

### Why Health Insurance Tax Estimators Are Essential for Financial Planning

Securing adequate health insurance coverage protects families against escalating medical inflation, but it also offers significant financial benefits through income tax savings. Under **Section 80D of the Income Tax Act**, taxpayers can claim deductions for health insurance premiums paid for themselves, their spouse, dependent children, and parents. An health insurance cover & tax benefit estimator calculates eligible deductions and direct tax savings instantly based on age brackets and tax slabs. Our browser-based tool computes tax calculations locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Estimate Your Section 80D Tax Savings in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select Age Groups: Choose whether you and your parents are below 60 years or senior citizens (60+ years).
  2. Enter Premiums: Input the annual health insurance premium amounts paid for your family and parents.
  3. Select Tax Slab: Choose your applicable income tax bracket (5%, 20%, or 30%) to view your actual cash tax savings immediately.
### Tax Deduction Limits + Worked Example

Section 80D limits vary depending on age categories for self/family and parents:

Max Self Deduction = ₹25,000 (or ₹50,000 if senior)  |  Max Parents Deduction = ₹25,000 (or ₹50,000 if senior)

Worked Example: Paying ₹20,000 for self/family (under 60) and ₹45,000 for senior citizen parents qualifies for a total eligible deduction of ₹75,000. Under a 30% tax slab, this yields direct annual tax savings of ₹22,500.
