How UAE, Saudi Arabia & GCC VAT Calculations, 5% and 15% Tax Rates Work

### Why GCC VAT Calculators Are Essential for Businesses and Consumers in the UAE and KSA

Following the historic GCC Framework Agreement, the United Arab Emirates (**UAE**) and Saudi Arabia (**KSA**) implemented Value Added Tax (**VAT**) to diversify government revenues. While the UAE maintains a standard rate of **5%**, Saudi Arabia adjusted its standard VAT rate to **15%**. Businesses registered with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) in the UAE or Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) in KSA must accurately add tax to sales invoices or extract VAT amounts from inclusive totals. A UAE & Saudi Arabia VAT calculator enables businesses and individuals to compute tax calculations instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates tax liabilities locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate GCC VAT in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select Country & Rate: Choose your target GCC nation (UAE 5% or Saudi Arabia 15%) from the dropdown menu.
  2. Select Calculation Mode: Switch between adding VAT (exclusive to inclusive) and removing VAT (inclusive to exclusive).
  3. Enter Amount & Review: Type your monetary amount to instantly inspect the exclusive figure, exact tax component, and grand total.
### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

Adding VAT multiplies exclusive amounts by $(1 + \text{rate})$, while removing VAT divides inclusive totals by $(1 + \text{rate})$:

VAT Inclusive = Ex-VAT * (1 + Rate)  |  VAT Amount = Inc-VAT - (Inc-VAT / (1 + Rate))

Worked Example (UAE 5%): Starting with an exclusive amount of AED 1,000 and adding 5% VAT results in **AED 50.00 in VAT** and a total inclusive amount of **AED 1,050.00**.

Worked Example (Saudi Arabia 15%): Starting with an exclusive amount of SAR 1,000 and adding 15% VAT results in **SAR 150.00 in VAT** and a total inclusive amount of **SAR 1,150.00**.
