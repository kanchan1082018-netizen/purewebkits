How Payment Gateway Fees, Merchant Processing & Markup Calculations Work

### Why Payment Gateway Fee Calculators Are Essential for Online Businesses

Accepting online payments through processors like Stripe, PayPal, or Razorpay involves merchant transaction fees consisting of a percentage rate plus a fixed per-transaction fee. Without proper accounting, these processing fees can quietly drain profit margins from e-commerce stores, SaaS subscriptions, and freelance invoices. A payment gateway fee calculator lets merchants instantly compute net earnings and determine exact markup amounts to pass processing fees onto customers. Our browser-based tool computes fees instantly in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Payment Processing Fees in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select Gateway: Choose your payment processor (Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, or custom rates) from the dropdown.
  2. Enter Amount: Type the total sale or invoice amount into the transaction amount box.
  3. View Net Payout & Markup: Instantly check the exact processing fee deducted, your net payout, and the gross amount to charge if you wish to cover fees.
### Processing Fee Formula + Worked Example

Gateway fees combine percentage rates with fixed transaction charges, while markup calculations invert the formula to cover fees:

Fee = (Amount * (Percentage / 100)) + Fixed  |  Net Payout = Amount - Fee  |  Markup = (Amount + Fixed) / (1 - (Percentage / 100))

Worked Example: For a $100 transaction on Stripe (2.9% + $0.30), the processing fee equals $3.20, leaving a net merchant payout of $96.80.
