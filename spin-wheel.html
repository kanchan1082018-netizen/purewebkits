Complete User Guide to the Online Spin Wheel, Crypto-Random Fairness & Giveaway Draws

How Equal-Probability Wheel Slicing & Cryptographic Randomness Work

Whether you are picking a giveaway winner on stream, calling on students in a classroom, assigning standup speakers, or breaking a tie on where to eat, a fair randomizer requires both equal geometric slice angles and unbiased random stop selection. When you enter N non-empty lines into the wheel, each entry occupies an exact central angle of 360° / N (2π / N radians) around the wheel perimeter, giving every entry an identical 1 / N probability of landing under the right-hand pointer. Instead of relying on predictable sequential seeds, the wheel uses the browser's built-in window.crypto.getRandomValues() cryptographic random number generator to pick an unbiased stopping angle combined with smooth cubic-ease-out deceleration and synthesized Web Audio peg ticks. A browser-based Spin Wheel runs 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

How to Customize & Spin the Wheel in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Enter Your Choices or Select a Preset: Type or paste one entry per line into Wheel Entries (One Per Line), or pick a built-in preset (Team / Names, Yes / No, Giveaway Prizes, or Numbers 1–10).
  2. Configure Spin Duration & Sound: Choose Quick (2.5s), Standard (4.2s), or Suspense (6.5s), toggle Web Audio peg ticks on or off, and optionally click Shuffle or Sort A–Z.
  3. Spin, Eliminate Winners & Export Log: Click Spin the Wheel (or click the wheel directly), click Remove Winner if running a multi-round elimination giveaway, and export results via Copy Winners or Download .txt.

Equal-Slice Probability & Angular Geometry Formula

For N active entries on the wheel:

Slice Angle = 360° / N  |  Single-Spin Win Probability = (1 / N) × 100%
Elimination Round k Probability (from initial N) = 1 / (N − k + 1)

Helpful Tips for Live Giveaways, Classrooms & Team Standups

  • Use "Remove Winner" for Multi-Prize Raffles: After a winner lands, click Remove Winner to automatically remove that entry from the list before spinning for the next prize.
  • Include Duplicate Lines for Weighted Odds: If one participant earned 2 raffle tickets while others earned 1, simply list that participant's name on two lines to double their slice share.
  • Click Anywhere on the Wheel Canvas to Spin: In addition to the main button, clicking directly on the wheel or center SPIN hub starts a spin immediately.

Worked Example: With 8 names entered on the wheel, each name occupies a 360° / 8 = 45° wedge and has an exact 1 / 8 = 12.50% probability on the first spin; removing the first winner leaves 7 entries at 51.43° (14.29%) each for Round 2.
