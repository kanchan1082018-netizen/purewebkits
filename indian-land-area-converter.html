How Indian Land Area Units, Bigha, Katha, Gaj & Acre Conversions Work

### Why Indian Land Area Converters Are Essential for Property Buyers and Farmers

Property transactions, agricultural planning, and real estate development in India often involve a complex mix of traditional regional units and metric measurements. Traditional units like **Bigha**, **Katha**, **Biswa**, and **Gaj (Square Yards)** vary significantly from one state to another (for instance, a Bigha in Uttar Pradesh differs from a Bigha in Bihar, Rajasthan, or Madhya Pradesh). Standardizing these units into universal metrics like **Acres**, **Hectares**, and **Square Feet** is crucial for accurate legal documentation and property valuation. An Indian land area converter translates traditional regional property measurements instantly. Our browser-based tool computes conversions locally in client memory without server uploads.

### Conversion Formula + Worked Example

Conversions rely on standard baseline equivalencies (where 1 Standard Bigha ≈ 27,225 sq. ft. or 0.625 Acres):

Square Feet = Input Value * Unit Base Multiplier  |  Converted Target Unit = Square Feet / Target Unit Divisor
