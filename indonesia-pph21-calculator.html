How Indonesian PPh 21 Income Tax, TER Withholding & PTKP Exemptions Work

### Why PPh 21 Calculators Are Essential for Employees and Employers in Indonesia

Employment income tax in Indonesia—officially known as **Pajak Penghasilan Pasal 21 (PPh 21)**—is withheld monthly by employers from employee salaries. Following recent Indonesian tax reforms (UU HPP), the Directorate General of Taxes introduced the **TER (Tarif Efektif Rata-rata / Average Effective Rate)** system to simplify monthly payroll withholding across categories A, B, and C based on PTKP (Penghasilan Tidak Kena Pajak / Non-Taxable Income) status. An Indonesia PPh 21 income tax calculator computes exact monthly withholding taxes and net take-home pay instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates tax deductions locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate PPh 21 Tax in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Monthly Gross Salary: Type your total monthly earnings including allowances before deductions.
  2. Select PTKP Category: Choose your correct TER category (A, B, or C) based on your marital status and dependents.
  3. Review Tax & Take-Home Pay: Inspect your monthly withholding tax percentage (TER %) and final net salary immediately.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Monthly PPh 21 tax under the TER system multiplies gross monthly salary by the applicable effective tax rate percentage:

Monthly PPh 21 Tax = Monthly Gross Salary * (TER Percentage / 100)  |  Take-Home Pay = Gross Salary - Monthly Tax

Worked Example: Earning a monthly gross salary of Rp 15,000,000 with a K/1 status (Category B, 9% TER rate) results in a monthly withholding tax of **Rp 1,350,000**, leaving a **Net Take-Home Pay of Rp 13,650,000**.
