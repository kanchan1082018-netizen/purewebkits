How Browser-Based PDF Splitting & Page Extraction Works

Why Separate or Extract Specific Pages Locally?

Large multi-page PDFs—such as 50-page bank statements, scanned legal binders, textbook chapters, or multi-invoice accounting batches—often contain far more pages than a recipient or upload portal requires. Submitting unnecessary pages slows down uploads, breaches file-size caps, and exposes unrelated confidential records. By using a 100% client-side PDF splitter powered by pdf-lib, your browser copies only the exact page tree objects you request into a brand-new PDF container. Zero pages are transmitted to external servers, and 100% of original vector fonts, form fields, and image sharpness stay completely intact.

How to Split a PDF Online in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Select Your PDF: Drag and drop any single- or multi-page .pdf file into the tool above to inspect its total page count.
  2. Choose a Split Mode: Select Extract Page Range to pull specific pages into one PDF, Split Every Page (ZIP) to burst every page into its own separate file, or Split by Fixed Interval to divide a large packet into equal multi-page chunks.
  3. Download Instantly: Click Split PDF Now to build your extracted PDF or ZIP archive locally in milliseconds.

Fixed-Interval Chunk Calculation Formula & Worked Example

When dividing a large document into equal batches of N pages per file, the total number of output PDFs generated inside your ZIP archive is determined by the ceiling division formula:

Total Split Files = ceil( Total Pages / Pages Per Split File )

Worked Example: Suppose you scan a stack of 24 two-sided client invoices into a single 48-page PDF, and each individual invoice spans exactly 2 pages.

By selecting Split by Fixed Interval and setting Pages Per Split File = 2, the splitter computes: ceil(48 / 2) = 24 separate 2-page PDF files, neatly numbered (part-01.pdf through part-24.pdf) and bundled inside a single ready-to-download ZIP file.
