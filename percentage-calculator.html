How Percentage Formulas, Ratios & Percentage Change Work

Why Percentages Are the Universal Language of Finance, Math & Analytics

The word percent comes from the Latin phrase per centum, meaning "by the hundred." A percentage expresses any fraction, ratio, or rate on a standardized scale out of 100—making it effortless to compare numbers of completely different magnitudes. Whether you are calculating a 20% retail discount, converting exam marks out of 500 into a percentage grade, measuring year-over-year revenue growth, or computing loan interest and stock portfolio returns, virtually every everyday calculation relies on one of three core percentage equations included in our 3-in-1 tool above.

The 3 Core Percentage Mathematical Formulas & Worked Example

Each of the three calculators above runs on a standard algebraic identity:

1) X% of Y = (X / 100) × Y
2) X as a % of Y = (X / Y) × 100
3) % Change = [ (New Value − Old Value) / |Old Value| ] × 100

Worked Example (Percentage Change): Suppose an e-commerce store's monthly orders grow from 1,200 orders in October to 1,500 orders in November.

Net Order Difference = 1,500 − 1,200 = +300 orders.

Percentage Growth = (300 / 1,200) × 100 = +25.00% increase. Conversely, if orders fell from 1,500 back to 1,200, the percentage drop would be (−300 / 1,500) × 100 = −20.00%.
