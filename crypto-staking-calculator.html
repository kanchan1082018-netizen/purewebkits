How Cryptocurrency Staking, APY Returns & Passive Income Work

### Why Crypto Staking & Reward Calculators Are Essential for Investors

In the digital asset ecosystem, Proof-of-Stake (PoS) blockchains allow cryptocurrency holders to lock their tokens into validating networks, securing transactions while earning passive income rewards. Unlike traditional savings accounts, crypto yields are often expressed as Annual Percentage Yield (APY) which incorporates compound interest over time. A crypto staking APY and reward calculator projects future token rewards and fiat portfolio values instantly. Our browser-based tool computes staking returns locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Staking Rewards in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Staked Amount: Type the total number of cryptocurrency tokens you plan to stake.
  2. Enter Coin Price & APY: Input the current token market price and the protocol's annual percentage yield (APY %).
  3. Set Duration: Specify the staking duration in days to inspect your total coin rewards and fiat portfolio growth immediately.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Staking rewards calculate daily proportional yield based on the staked principal and annual percentage yield:

Reward Coins = Staked Amount * (APY / 100) * (Lock Days / 365)

Worked Example: Staking 1,000 coins valued at $150 each with an APY of 12% over 365 days yields **120.00 reward coins**, valued at **$18,000**, bringing your ending portfolio value to **$168,000**.
