How UAE End-of-Service Gratuity Calculation Works Under UAE Labour Law

Key Rules Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021

Under Article 51 of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (effective February 2022 across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and all mainland private-sector employers), every full-time expatriate employee who completes at least 1 full year of continuous service is entitled to an end-of-service gratuity payment upon resignation or termination. Unlike older UAE labour laws that reduced gratuity for employees who resigned early on unlimited contracts, the updated UAE Labour Law grants 100% of the accrued gratuity regardless of whether the employee resigns or is terminated—provided the minimum 1-year service threshold is met.

How to Calculate Your UAE Gratuity in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Enter Monthly Basic Salary: Input only your Basic Salary in AED from your MOHRE employment contract—excluding housing, transportation, phone, or utility allowances.
  2. Enter Service Duration or Exact Dates: Type your completed years, months, and days—or switch to Select Joining & Last Working Dates and subtract any unpaid leave days.
  3. Review 21-Day & 30-Day Breakdown: View your daily basic wage, first 5-year indemnity (21 days/year), post-5-year indemnity (30 days/year), and statutory 2-year total wage cap check.

UAE Gratuity Mathematical Formula & Worked Example

MOHRE calculates daily basic wage by dividing monthly basic salary by 30 calendar days, then applies two progressive tiers:

Daily Basic Wage = Monthly Basic / 30  |  Years 1–5: 21 Days/Yr  |  Years 5+: 30 Days/Yr

Worked Example: Suppose an engineer in Dubai earns a Monthly Basic Salary of AED 12,000 (Total package AED 18,000) and completes exactly 7 years of continuous service with zero unpaid leave.

Daily Basic Wage = AED 12,000 / 30 = AED 400.00 per day.

First 5 Years = 5 × 21 days × AED 400 = AED 42,000. Remaining 2 Years = 2 × 30 days × AED 400 = AED 24,000. Total Payable Gratuity = AED 66,000.00.
