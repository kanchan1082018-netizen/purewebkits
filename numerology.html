Complete User Guide to Numerology Core Numbers, Pythagorean vs. Chaldean Charts & Master Numbers

How Life Path, Destiny, Soul Urge, and Personality Numbers Are Derived

Classical numerology reduces dates and alphabetical letter values into core single-digit archetypes (1 through 9) alongside the three traditional Master Numbers (11, 22, and 33). Your Life Path Number (often paired with your Birthday / Mulank Number from the calendar day of birth) is calculated by reducing your birth month, birth day, and four-digit birth year separately and summing those reduced components—preserving 11, 22, or 33 whenever they appear. Your name numbers—Expression / Destiny Number (all letters), Soul Urge / Heart's Desire Number (vowels A, E, I, O, U), and Personality Number (consonants)—map each letter to a digit using either the sequential Pythagorean (1–9) alphabet chart or the ancient vibrational Chaldean (1–8) chart (where 9 is reserved and not assigned to any letter). A browser-based Numerology Number Calculator computes both systems 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.
