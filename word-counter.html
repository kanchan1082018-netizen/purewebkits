Reading Time & Speaking Time Formulas + Worked Example

Editorial platforms and speechwriters calculate estimated audience time using standard adult comprehension benchmarks—225 words per minute (WPM) for silent reading and 130 WPM for clear public speaking:

Reading Time (Minutes) = Total Words / 225  |  Speaking Time (Minutes) = Total Words / 130

Worked Example: Suppose you write an informative blog guide containing exactly 1,350 words and 8,420 characters.

Estimated Silent Reading Time = 1,350 / 225 = 6.0 minutes (displayed as 6 min 0 sec).

Estimated Video / Podcast Speaking Time = 1,350 / 130 = 10.38 minutes (approximately 10 min 23 sec at a natural narration pace).
