Complete User Guide to Converting CGPA to Percentage Across 10-Point and 4-Point Scales

Why Universities, Employers, and Application Portals Ask for Both CGPA and Percentage

Many schools, colleges, and universities report academic performance as a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) or Grade Point Average (GPA) on a 10-point or 4-point scale, while scholarship forms, job applications, admissions portals, and exam eligibility criteria frequently request a percentage. Because different boards and institutions use slightly different conversion formulas—such as multiplying by 9.5 in traditional CBSE/10-point conversions, multiplying directly by 10, applying an institution-specific offset like (CGPA − 0.75) × 10, or scaling a 4.0 GPA to 100%—picking the right formula matters. A browser-based CGPA to Percentage Calculator runs these conversions 100% locally in your browser using JavaScript—with zero server uploads.

How to Convert CGPA to Percentage (or Percentage to CGPA) in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Choose Conversion Direction: Select CGPA → Percentage or Percentage → CGPA at the top of the studio, or click Load Sample (8.5 CGPA).
  2. Enter Score & Grading Scale: Type your CGPA or percentage and choose the grading scale formula that matches your board, university, or application instructions.
  3. Review Standing & Export: Inspect your converted score, academic standing tier, and scale share, then click Copy Result or Download .txt.

Common CGPA & Percentage Conversion Formulas

Depending on the selected scale preset:

10-Point (9.5x): Percentage = CGPA × 9.5  |  10-Point Direct: Percentage = CGPA × 10
AICTE Offset: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10  |  4-Point GPA: Percentage = (GPA / 4.0) × 100

Helpful Tips for Academic Applications

  • Always Check Your University or Board Ordinance: If your transcript or prospectus specifies a particular conversion rule, use that exact formula rather than assuming a single universal multiplier.
  • Verify Rounding Rules: Check whether your application portal requires two decimal places (e.g., 80.75%) or rounded values.
  • Use Reverse Mode for Target Planning: Switch to Percentage → CGPA to see what CGPA corresponds to a target percentage cutoff.

Worked Example: Converting a CGPA of 8.50 on a 10-point scale using CGPA × 9.5 gives 8.50 × 9.5 = 80.75%.
