How Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR), Investment Returns & Compounding Work

### Why CAGR Calculators Are Essential for Investors

Evaluating investment performance over multiple years can be challenging when annual returns fluctuate wildly up and down. Simply taking the average of yearly returns often misleads investors about true long-term growth. The **Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR)** measures the smoothed annual rate of return of an investment over a specified period longer than one year, assuming profits are reinvested at the end of each year. A **CAGR calculator** computes absolute gains and compound annual growth rates instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates financial returns locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate CAGR in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Initial Value: Type your starting investment principal into the initial value field.
  2. Enter Final Value: Type your ending portfolio or asset value into the final value field.
  3. Enter Duration: Specify the total number of years the investment was held to inspect your CAGR percentage immediately.
### CAGR Formula + Worked Example

CAGR divides final value by initial value, raises the result to the power of 1 divided by years, and subtracts 1:

CAGR = (Final Value / Initial Value)^(1 / Years) - 1

Worked Example: Growing an initial investment of $100,000 to $250,000 over 5 years yields an absolute profit of $150,000 (150% absolute return) and a **CAGR of 20.11%** per year.
