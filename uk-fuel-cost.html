Complete User Guide to UK Fuel Cost, L/100km, and UK vs. US MPG Conversions

Why UK Drivers Switch Between Miles, Liters, L/100km, and MPG

Fuel calculations in the United Kingdom blend imperial and metric conventions: road distances and dashboard fuel economy are typically shown in miles and imperial miles per gallon (UK MPG), whereas fuel at the pump is sold by the liter in pounds sterling (£/L), and international vehicle specs often quote consumption in liters per 100 kilometers (L/100km). Because an imperial gallon (4.54609 L) is larger than a US gallon (3.78541 L), converting between UK MPG, US MPG, and L/100km requires exact gallon constants. UK Fuel Cost, L/100km & MPG Conversion Formulas

Using 1 mi = 1.609344 km and 1 UK imperial gallon = 4.54609 L:

L/100km = 282.480936 / UK_MPG  |  UK_MPG = 282.480936 / (L/100km)
Liters Needed = (Distance_km / 100) × (L/100km)  |  Trip Cost = Liters Needed × Price_per_Liter

Helpful Tips for Accurate Fuel Budgeting

  • Check Real-World vs. Lab Figures: Highway speeds, hills, traffic, cargo weight, and air conditioning affect real-world fuel economy compared to official WLTP/lab ratings.
  • Never Confuse UK MPG With US MPG: 45 UK MPG equals roughly 37.47 US MPG (~6.28 L/100km).
  • Use Exact Forecourt Prices: Petrol and diesel prices vary by station and motorway vs. supermarket forecourts.

Worked Example: Driving 100 miles (160.93 km) at 45 UK MPG (~6.28 L/100km) uses about 8.99 liters of fuel, costing roughly £13.31 at £1.48/L.
