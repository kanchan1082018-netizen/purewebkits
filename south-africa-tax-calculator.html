How South African PAYE Income Tax, SARS Tax Brackets & 15% VAT Work

### Why South African Tax & VAT Calculators Are Essential for Workers and Businesses

In South Africa, employment earnings are subject to Pay-As-You-Earn (**PAYE**) income tax, which is withheld monthly by employers on behalf of the South African Revenue Service (**SARS**). PAYE tax calculations factor in progressive tax brackets, primary rebates, and age-based secondary or tertiary rebates. Additionally, businesses and consumers must account for the standard **15% Value Added Tax (VAT)** on goods and services. A South Africa PAYE income tax & VAT calculator evaluates both employee salary tax deductions and 15% VAT amounts instantly. Our browser-based tool computes tax liabilities locally in client memory without server uploads.

### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

PAYE tax applies progressive tax brackets minus SARS tax rebates, while VAT adds or extracts 15%:

Net Pay = Gross Salary - Annual PAYE  |  VAT Amount (Inclusive) = Total - (Total / 1.15)

Worked Example: Earning an annual gross salary of R450,000 under age 65 results in a SARS PAYE tax liability of **R89,310**, leaving a **Net Take-Home Pay of R360,690**. For VAT, extracting 15% from R1,150 yields **R150.00 in VAT** and **R1,000.00 ex-VAT**.
