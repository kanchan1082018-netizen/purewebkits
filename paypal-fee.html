Complete User Guide to PayPal Fee Calculation, Net Payouts & Invoice Gross-Up

How Percentage + Fixed Transaction Fees and Gross-Up Pricing Work

Standard commercial payment fees combine a percentage fee (calculated on the transaction amount) with a fixed per-transaction fee (such as $0.49 or $0.30 in USD). Because published fee rates differ by country, account type, currency, and payment method—and may also be accompanied by currency conversion spreads or applicable local taxes on fees—entering your exact percentage and fixed fee into a calculator gives you clear visibility before sending an invoice or pricing a product. In Gross → Net Payout mode, you start with the gross amount a buyer pays and see how much remains after fees; in Target Net → Gross-Up mode, you enter the net amount you want to keep and compute the higher gross invoice total to charge so your take-home payout hits your target. How to Calculate PayPal Fees & Gross-Up Invoices in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Choose Gross → Net or Target Net → Gross-Up: Select Gross → Net Payout to check what you receive from a given charge, or Target Net → Gross-Up to find how much to invoice for a desired net payout.
  2. Enter Amount & Fee Rate (or Pick a Preset): Type your amount, percentage rate, and fixed fee—or click one of the rate preset chips and customize any figure to match your account's exact schedule.
  3. Review Breakdown & Export Summary: Check total fee, gross amount, net received, and effective fee percentage, then save via Copy Fee Breakdown or Download .txt.

Forward Fee & Reverse Gross-Up Formulas

With decimal rate r = percent / 100 and fixed fee F:

Forward (Gross G → Net N): Fee = (G × r) + F  |  Net (N) = G − Fee
Reverse (Target Net N → Gross G): G = (N + F) / (1 − r)

Helpful Tips for Pricing Invoices & Small Transactions

  • Watch Fixed-Fee Drag on Small Payments: On a small $10.00 payment, a fixed $0.49 fee equals 4.90% on its own before adding any percentage fee—factoring this into small-ticket pricing protects your margin.
  • Account for FX Conversion & Local Taxes Separately: If a payment involves currency conversion or local tax on service fees in your region, add any expected conversion markup or tax adjustment on top of base transaction fees.
  • Verify Your Exact Account Rate Schedule: Always confirm your current country and account-specific fee terms before finalizing client invoices.

Worked Example: To receive $500.00 net at 3.49% + $0.49, reverse gross-up calculation gives ($500.00 + $0.49) / (1 − 0.0349) = $500.49 / 0.9651 ≈ $518.59 gross—resulting in a $18.59 fee and $500.00 net received.
