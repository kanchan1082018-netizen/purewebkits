Complete User Guide to Forex Pip Value, Lot Sizes (Standard, Mini, Micro) & Account Currency Conversion

What Is a Pip and How Lot Size Determines Monetary Risk per Pip

In foreign exchange (Forex) trading, a pip ("percentage in point" or "price interest point") is the standard unit of price movement for a currency pair. For most currency pairs quoted to four or five decimal places (such as EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, or USD/CHF), 1 pip = 0.0001 (the fourth decimal place, while the fifth decimal place is a fractional pipette equal to 0.1 pip). For Japanese Yen pairs (such as USD/JPY, EUR/JPY, or GBP/JPY) and USD/INR quoted to two or three decimal places, 1 pip = 0.01 (the second decimal place). Knowing your exact pip value in your account currency lets you connect stop-loss distance in pips directly to dollar risk before opening a position. Forex Pip Value Formula

Let Units = Lots × 100,000, PipSize be 0.0001 (or 0.01 for JPY/INR pairs), and R_quote_to_acct be the conversion rate from 1 unit of the quote currency into your account currency:

Pip Value (Quote Currency) = Units × PipSize
Pip Value (Account Currency) = (Units × PipSize) × R_quote_to_acct

Helpful Tips for Forex Risk & Lot Sizing

  • USD-Quoted Pairs Have Fixed USD Pip Values: When trading EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, or NZD/USD in a USD account, 1.00 standard lot is always $10.00/pip, 0.10 mini lot is $1.00/pip, and 0.01 micro lot is $0.10/pip.
  • Base-USD Pairs Scale With the Exchange Rate: For pairs like USD/JPY or USD/CAD in a USD account, R_quote_to_acct = 1 / ExchangeRate, so pip value shifts slightly as the pair's price moves.
  • Educational Math Tool Only: This calculator provides Forex arithmetic and risk modeling only and is not financial or investment advice.

Worked Example: Trading 1.00 standard lot (100,000 units) of EUR/USD (PipSize = 0.0001) in a USD account gives 100,000 × 0.0001 = $10.00 per pip ($1.00/pip for a 0.10 mini lot and $0.10/pip for a 0.01 micro lot).
