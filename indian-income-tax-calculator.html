Comprehensive Guide to Indian Income Tax Slabs, New vs. Old Regimes, and Deductions

### Why Indian Income Tax Calculators Are Essential for Salaried Employees and Professionals

Filing annual income tax returns and managing payroll deductions in India requires careful comparison between the **New Tax Regime** and the **Old Tax Regime**. Introduced to simplify taxation and lower tax rates across middle-income brackets, the New Tax Regime offers concessional slab rates but eliminates most traditional tax deductions and exemptions (such as Section 80C, 80D, HRA, and LTA). Conversely, the Old Tax Regime retains these popular investment and expense deductions, which can significantly reduce taxable income for taxpayers who maximize their Section 80C, health insurance, and home loan interest exemptions. Deciding which regime is more financially advantageous depends entirely on your total gross income and the quantum of eligible deductions you can claim. Manually calculating tax liabilities across both systems—incorporating standard deductions, rebate limits under Section 87A, and the 4% Health and Education Cess—can be complex and error-prone. A professional **Indian Income Tax Calculator** enables salaried individuals, freelancers, and taxpayers to input gross annual incomes and deduction totals to compare tax liabilities under both regimes instantly. Our browser-based utility calculates tax slabs locally in client memory without external server uploads, guaranteeing absolute data privacy and lightning-fast execution.

### Income Tax Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Income tax liabilities are computed by applying progressive slab rates to taxable income, subtracting applicable tax rebates under Section 87A, and adding a 4% Health and Education Cess:

$\text{Taxable Income (Old)} = \text{Gross Income} - \text{Standard Deduction} - \sum \text{Deductions (80C, 80D, etc.)}$  |  $\text{Total Tax} = \text{Calculated Tax} \times 1.04$

Worked Example: Earn a gross salary of **₹12,00,000** with **₹2,25,000 in total deductions** (80C, 80D, HRA). Under the New Regime, after the ₹75,000 standard deduction, the tax liability is **₹46,800**. Under the Old Regime, taxable income is reduced to ₹9,00,000, resulting in a tax liability of **₹62,400**. The **New Regime saves ₹15,600** in this scenario.
