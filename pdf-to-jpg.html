How PDF-to-JPG Rasterization, DPI Resolution & Privacy Protection Work

Why Converting PDF Pages to JPG or PNG Is Essential for Sharing & Uploads

While PDF (Portable Document Format) stores vector fonts and multi-page layouts cleanly, many government job portals, university admission forms, social media platforms (Instagram, WhatsApp Status, LinkedIn carousels), and presentation decks only accept raster image uploads in .jpg or .png format. Instead of sending confidential IDs, bank statements, or signed contracts to an unknown cloud server, our converter uses Mozilla's PDF.js engine and HTML5 Canvas to render every PDF page directly inside your own browser's memory at selectable 108 DPI, 144 DPI, or 216 DPI clarity.

PDF Point-to-Pixel DPI Formula & Worked Example

PDF page dimensions are defined in typographic points (where 1 inch = 72 points), so exported image pixel width and height scale directly with your chosen DPI:

Output Pixel Width = ( PDF Width in Points / 72 ) × Target DPI

Worked Example: A standard international A4 PDF page measures 595 × 842 points (8.27 × 11.69 inches).

At our default 2.0x render scale (144 DPI), exported pixel width = (595 / 72) × 144 = 1,190 px and height = (842 / 72) × 144 = 1,684 px.

Switching to 3.0x scale (216 DPI) produces a crisp 1,785 × 2,526 px image suitable for high-resolution printing and OCR text extraction.
