Complete User Guide to Stone & Pounds (st & lb) to Kilograms (kg) Conversion

Why Stones and Pounds Are Common in the UK and Ireland Alongside Metric Kilograms

In the United Kingdom and Ireland, body weight and certain everyday weights are frequently expressed in stones and pounds (for example, 11 st 4 lb), whereas international health charts, fitness apps, sports categories, and scientific scales use kilograms (kg). Because one stone equals exactly 14 pounds and one international avoirdupois pound equals 0.45359237 kilograms, one stone equals 6.35029318 kilograms. Converting mixed stones-and-pounds values into kilograms—or splitting a decimal kilogram weight back into whole stones plus remaining pounds—can be tedious by hand. A browser-based Stone & Pounds to KG Converter performs both directions instantaneously—100% locally in your browser with zero server uploads.

Exact Stone, Pound & Kilogram Conversion Formulas

Using the exact international pound definition (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg) and 1 stone = 14 lb:

Total Pounds = (Stones × 14) + Pounds  |  Kilograms = Total Pounds × 0.45359237
Total Stones = Kilograms / (14 × 0.45359237)  |  Remainder lb = (Total Stones − floor(Total Stones)) × 14

Helpful Tips for Accurate Weight Conversions

  • Avoid Rounding Early Between Steps: Keep full precision until the final display step so converting back and forth stays accurate.
  • Check Whether Pounds Are Whole or Decimal: The converter supports decimal pounds (such as 4.5 lb) as well as whole pounds.
  • Use Grams for Fine Precision: Because 1 kg = 1,000 g, the grams readout gives a fine-grained metric view.

Worked Example: 11 stones 4 pounds equals (11 × 14) + 4 = 158 pounds. Multiplying 158 by 0.45359237 gives approximately 71.67 kg (71,667.6 g).
