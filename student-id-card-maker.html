Complete User Guide to Student ID Card Design, Multi-Color Templates & CR80 Print Sizing

How Standard CR80 ID Card Dimensions, Optional Passport Photos & Verification Codes Work

Schools, colleges, coaching institutes, libraries, and training academies rely on clear, well-structured student ID cards to identify enrolled learners, display emergency guardian contact numbers and blood groups, and streamline campus entry. Standard PVC and laminated ID cards follow the international ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 (CR80) dimension of 2.125 × 3.375 inches (53.98 × 85.60 mm), which renders at 638 × 1012 pixels in Vertical (Portrait) orientation or 1012 × 638 pixels in Horizontal (Landscape) orientation at crisp 300 DPI print resolution. Because some institutions require a passport-size student photograph while temporary passes, exam hall cards, or library cards may omit photos, you can upload a student photo or switch seamlessly to a clean No Photo Layout without broken boxes. Choosing from 25 multi-color sample templates and customizing header colors, accent bands, text colors, fonts, QR codes, and barcode strips gives you complete design control. A browser-based Student ID Card Maker renders everything 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

CR80 ID Card Print Dimension Formula

Converting standard CR80 physical card dimensions into 300 DPI print pixels:

Portrait CR80: 2.125 in × 300 DPI = 638 px (W)  ×  3.375 in × 300 DPI = 1012 px (H)
Landscape CR80: 3.375 in × 300 DPI = 1012 px (W)  ×  2.125 in × 300 DPI = 638 px (H)

Helpful Tips for Clean School & College ID Card Printing

  • Use Bright, Evenly Lit Passport Photos: When uploading a student photo, pick a front-facing portrait with a clean background so facial features stay sharp inside the passport frame.
  • Keep Emergency Phone & Blood Group Visible: Displaying a guardian phone number and blood group directly on the front helps staff respond quickly during school trips or campus emergencies.
  • Print at 100% Actual Size for PVC Pouches & Lamination: Ensure your print dialog is set to 100% scale so the printed card fits standard CR80 badge holders and lanyards cleanly.
