How Crypto Futures Leverage, Liquidation Prices & Risk Management Work

### Why Crypto Futures & Liquidation Calculators Are Essential for Traders

Cryptocurrency futures trading allows traders to amplify their buying or selling power using **leverage**, opening positions significantly larger than their initial collateral (margin). While leverage can exponentially multiply trading gains, it similarly magnifies potential losses. If market prices move against an open position beyond the available collateral threshold, the exchange forcibly closes the trade—a process known as **liquidation**. A crypto futures leverage and liquidation calculator computes exact liquidation prices and position sizing instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates trading risk metrics locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Futures Liquidation in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select Position Type: Choose whether you are opening a Long (buy expecting price rise) or Short (sell expecting price fall) position.
  2. Enter Entry Price & Leverage: Input your coin entry price and select your leverage multiplier (e.g., 10x, 20x, 50x).
  3. Enter Collateral Margin: Type your initial margin amount to inspect your total position size and exact liquidation price immediately.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Liquidation price formulas account for entry price, leverage, and maintenance margin buffers:

Long Liquidation = Entry Price * (1 - (1 / Leverage) + Maintenance Margin Rate)

Worked Example: Opening a Long position on Bitcoin at $90,000 with 20x leverage ($1,000 margin = $20,000 position size) results in an estimated liquidation price of **$85,725.00** (a 4.75% adverse price move).
