How Australian Goods and Services Tax (GST), 10% Calculations & BAS Work

### Why Australian GST Calculators Are Essential for Businesses and Consumers

In Australia, the **Goods and Services Tax (GST)** is a broad-based 10% tax levied on most goods, services, and other items sold or consumed in the country. Businesses registered for GST must add 10% to their taxable sales (GST inclusive) and can claim credits for GST included in their business purchases, reporting these figures to the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) via Business Activity Statements (BAS). A Australian GST calculator allows users to quickly add 10% GST to exclusive prices or extract 10% GST from inclusive totals. Our browser-based tool calculates tax computations locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Australian GST in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select Mode: Choose whether you want to **Add 10% GST** (exclusive to inclusive) or **Remove 10% GST** (inclusive to exclusive).
  2. Enter Amount: Type your target dollar amount into the input field.
  3. Review Breakdown: Instantly check the exclusive amount, exact 10% GST component, and inclusive total.
### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

Adding GST multiplies the exclusive amount by 1.10, whereas removing GST divides the inclusive amount by 1.10:

GST Inclusive = Excl * 1.10  |  GST Amount = Incl - (Incl / 1.10)  |  Ex-GST = Incl / 1.10

Worked Example: Starting with an exclusive amount of A$1,000 and adding 10% GST results in **A$100.00 in GST** and a total inclusive amount of **A$1,100.00**. Conversely, extracting GST from A$1,100 inclusive yields A$1,000 ex-GST and A$100 GST.
