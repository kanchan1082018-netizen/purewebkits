How SHA-256, SHA-512 Cryptographic Hashing & Security Standards Work

Why Secure Hash Algorithms (SHA-2) Are Vital for Modern Cryptography

The SHA-2 (Secure Hash Algorithm 2) cryptographic hash family—comprising SHA-256 and SHA-512—was designed by the National Security Agency (NSA) to provide robust, collision-resistant message digests. While older algorithms like MD5 have been compromised by collision vulnerabilities, SHA-256 and SHA-512 are mathematically secure standards utilized across blockchain networks (like Bitcoin), TLS/SSL certificates, digital signatures, and secure file verification. SHA-256 & SHA-512 Digest Formulas + Worked Example
