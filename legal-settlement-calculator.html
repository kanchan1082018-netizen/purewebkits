How Legal Settlements, Contingency Fees & Payout Distributions Work

### Why Legal Settlement Calculators Are Essential for Claimants

When resolving a personal injury claim, auto accident lawsuit, or civil dispute, understanding how settlement funds are distributed is critical. Most personal injury attorneys operate on a **contingency fee basis**, meaning they receive a predetermined percentage of the recovery only if the case wins or settles successfully. However, the gross settlement amount does not go straight to the client; deductions must first be made for attorney fees, litigation expenses (expert witness fees, filing fees, medical record costs), and medical liens or outstanding healthcare bills. A legal settlement & contingency fee calculator computes exact net client payouts instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates distribution breakdowns locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Settlement Payouts in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Gross Settlement: Type the total recovery or settlement amount awarded in your case.
  2. Enter Contingency Fee %: Input your attorney's agreed percentage fee (typically 33% pre-trial or 40% if trial ensues).
  3. Enter Expenses & Liens: Input your litigation costs and outstanding medical bills to reveal your final net client payout immediately.
### Calculation Formula + Worked Example

Net settlement disbursement subtracts attorney fees, case expenses, and medical liens from the gross recovery:

Attorney Fee = Settlement * (Fee% / 100)  |  Net Payout = Settlement - Attorney Fee - Expenses - Liens

Worked Example: Receiving a $150,000 settlement with a 33.3% contingency fee ($49,950), $5,000 in litigation expenses, and $15,000 in medical liens results in total deductions of $69,950, leaving a **Net Client Payout of $80,050**.
