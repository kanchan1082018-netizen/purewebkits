Complete User Guide to Google AdSense Page RPM, Impression RPM, Page CTR & CPC

How Page RPM Differs From Impression RPM and CPC

In Google AdSense and display ad monetization, Page RPM (Revenue Per Mille / per 1,000 pageviews) represents your estimated earnings for every 1,000 pageviews your site receives: Page RPM = (Estimated Earnings / Pageviews) × 1,000. Because a single web page often renders multiple ad slots (such as a top banner, in-content unit, and sidebar unit), total ad impressions typically exceed pageviews by a factor of 2x to 4x—making Impression RPM ((Estimated Earnings / Total Ad Impressions) × 1,000) lower per unit than Page RPM. Underneath both RPM figures sits the publisher monetization identity: Page RPM = Page CTR (%) × CPC × 10, showing that higher-value advertiser niches (higher CPC) and well-placed, viewable ad slots (higher Page CTR) work together to lift overall RPM. AdSense Page RPM, Impression RPM & Publisher Revenue Formulas

Let PV be pageviews, E be estimated earnings, Clicks be ad clicks, and AdsPerPage be average ad impressions per pageview:

Page RPM = (E / PV) × 1,000  |  Impression RPM = (E / (PV × AdsPerPage)) × 1,000
Page CTR (%) = (Clicks / PV) × 100  |  CPC = E / Clicks  |  Page RPM = Page CTR (%) × CPC × 10

Helpful Tips for Improving Publisher Page RPM Sustainably

  • Prioritize High-Intent Content Niches & Geo Mix: Advertiser CPC varies significantly by topic (finance, software, B2B, and insurance typically bid higher than general entertainment) and visitor geography.
  • Optimize Viewability & Core Web Vitals Rather Than Ad Clutter: Clean layouts with strong above-the-fold viewability and fast page loads protect user experience and policy compliance while lifting active-view bids.
  • Avoid Encouraging Accidental Clicks: Keep clear spacing between interactive buttons/calculators and ad units to prevent invalid click deductions.

Worked Example: Generating $450.00 across 100,000 pageviews and 1,500 ad clicks (with 3 ad impressions per page = 300,000 impressions) yields a Page RPM of ($450 / 100,000) × 1,000 = $4.50, an Impression RPM of $1.50, a Page CTR of 1.50%, and an average CPC of $0.30.
