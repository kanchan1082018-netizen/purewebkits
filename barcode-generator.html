How Barcode Linear Symbologies, Encoding & Inventory Scanning Work

Why Barcode Generation Is Essential for Retail, POS and Supply Chain Management

Linear barcodes are optical machine-readable representations of data utilized globally across retail point-of-sale (POS) systems, warehouse inventory tracking, logistics, and asset management. Formats like CODE128, EAN-13, and UPC-A enable instant optical laser scanning of product serial numbers and SKUs. Our browser-based Barcode Generator uses the robust JsBarcode library to render crisp, scannable linear barcodes instantly in client memory without server uploads.

How to Generate and Download a Barcode in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Enter Data: Type your product SKU, numbers, or text string into the barcode data input box.
  2. Select Symbology: Choose your target barcode format (such as CODE128, EAN-13, or UPC) and customize colors and height.
  3. Download PNG: Click Download Barcode (PNG) to save your high-resolution barcode image file instantly.

Barcode Encoding & Symbology Standards Formula + Worked Example

Linear barcode generation converts alphanumeric strings into precise black-and-white bar-and-space widths based on symbology rules:

JsBarcode( "#barcodeSvg", InputText, { format: "CODE128", lineColor: "#0F172A", background: "#FFFFFF", width: 2, height: 80 } )

Worked Example: Encoding text "123456789012" using CODE128 format generates a precise retail barcode with automated checksum verification.
