Complete User Guide to Stripe Processing Fees, Net Payouts & Gross-Up Pricing

How Percentage + Fixed Per-Transaction Fees and Gross-Up Pricing Work

Card payment processors typically price online transactions using a combination of a percentage fee on the charge amount plus a fixed per-transaction fee (such as 2.90% + $0.30 for standard US domestic card charges, with additional percentage surcharges often applying to international cards, currency conversion, or optional add-ons). Because regional pricing, payment methods, and currency conversion terms vary by country and account configuration, entering your exact percentage and fixed fee lets you forecast exact payouts before launching a checkout page or sending a payment link. Switching to Target Net → Gross-Up calculates the higher gross charge amount needed so that after fees are deducted, your net payout equals your target goal. A browser-based Stripe Fee Calculator computes these figures 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

How to Calculate Processing Fees & Gross-Up Amounts in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Select Gross → Net Payout or Target Net → Gross-Up: Choose Gross → Net Payout to see what you receive from a given charge, or Target Net → Gross-Up to compute the customer charge required for a desired net payout.
  2. Enter Amount & Fee Rate (or Pick a Preset): Enter your amount, percentage rate, and fixed per-charge fee—or click one of the rate preset chips and customize any value.
  3. Inspect Breakdown & Export Summary: Check total fee, gross charge, net payout, and effective fee rate, then save via Copy Fee Breakdown or Download .txt.

Forward Fee & Reverse Gross-Up Formulas

With decimal rate r = percent / 100 and fixed fee F:

Forward (Gross G → Net N): Fee = (G × r) + F  |  Net (N) = G − Fee
Reverse (Target Net N → Gross G): G = (N + F) / (1 − r)

Helpful Tips for Checkout Pricing & Margin Protection

  • Account for Fixed-Fee Impact on Low-Ticket Orders: On a $5.00 charge, a $0.30 fixed fee alone represents 6.00% before any percentage rate—check margins carefully on low-priced items or subscriptions.
  • Include Currency Conversion & Add-On Fees When Applicable: If customers pay in a non-settlement currency or you enable extra verification/billing features, include any expected surcharge in your rate inputs.
  • Confirm Your Region's Published Rate Card: Card processing rates differ across countries and payment methods—always verify your exact account schedule.

Worked Example: On a $250.00 gross charge at 2.90% + $0.30, the percentage fee is $7.25 and the fixed fee is $0.30, giving a total fee of $7.55 (3.02% effective rate) and a net payout of $242.45.
