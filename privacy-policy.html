Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 — How PureWebKits protects your data and privacy.

Welcome to PureWebKits (PureWebKits.com). Your privacy is fundamental to our architecture and mission. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or use our suite of browser-based utilities, in compliance with Google AdSense requirements, the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1 100% Client-Side Processing Guarantee

Zero-Upload Promise: Every image compression, PDF editing, text conversion, developer formatting, and financial calculation tool on PureWebKits runs 100% locally inside your web browser using modern HTML5, JavaScript, and WebAssembly technologies.

Unlike traditional online utilities that upload your files to remote cloud servers for processing, PureWebKits operates strictly on the client side:

  • No File Uploads: Your images, PDF documents, text snippets, source code, and financial figures are never uploaded to, transmitted to, stored on, or viewed by PureWebKits servers.
  • Instant Local Cleanup: Once you close or refresh your browser tab, all temporary in-memory data used during processing is immediately cleared by your browser.
  • Total Confidentiality: Because your data never leaves your device, sensitive personal documents, business spreadsheets, and private media remain completely under your control.

2 Information We Collect

PureWebKits does not require user registration, accounts, or logins. We limit data collection strictly to what is necessary to operate securely and respond to direct inquiries:

A. Non-Personal Technical & Server Logs

Like most modern websites, our hosting and network security infrastructure (such as Cloudflare) automatically collects standard, non-personally-identifying technical metadata when you access PureWebKits.com. This may include:

  • Browser type, operating system, and device category (desktop/mobile).
  • Referring/exit pages, timestamps, and general country-level geographic region.
  • Anonymized or transient IP address logs used strictly for DDoS protection, rate limiting, spam prevention, and aggregate traffic analytics.

B. Voluntary Contact Information

If you choose to reach out to us via our Contact Page or send an email directly to website1082018@gmail.com, we collect your name, email address, and message content solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry, fixing reported bugs, or reviewing feature requests. We never sell, rent, or trade your email address with third parties.

3 Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To keep PureWebKits 100% free and accessible without paywalls or subscriptions, we display advertisements served by Google AdSense and authorized third-party advertising partners.

  • Use of Advertising Cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google DoubleClick / DART cookie) to serve ads to you based on your prior visits to PureWebKits.com and other websites across the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet, measuring ad performance and preventing repetitive ad displays.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising: You have full control over personalized ads. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com). Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu (for EU users).
  • Browser Cookie Controls: You may also instruct your web browser to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie is being sent. Please note that disabling cookies will not break our client-side tools, though ad relevance may change.

4 GDPR (European Users) & CCPA (California Users) Privacy Rights

PureWebKits respects global data protection standards and extends clear privacy rights to all visitors, including residents of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California.

European Economic Area & UK Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the following rights regarding any personal data we may hold (such as support emails you sent us):

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy of it.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that we correct inaccurate information or permanently delete your support correspondence.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Manage consent for non-essential advertising cookies via our consent prompts or your browser settings.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know What Data Is Collected: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected (which, for PureWebKits, is limited to voluntary support emails and standard automated web logs).
  • Request Deletion: Request deletion of any personal information we have collected from you.
  • Opt-Out of "Sale" or "Sharing": PureWebKits does not sell user data for monetary consideration. Where third-party advertising cookies constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under CCPA, you may opt out via Google Ads Settings or your browser's Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you or restrict access to any PureWebKits utility for exercising your privacy rights.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

PureWebKits is a general-audience utility website and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us via email or our contact form, please contact us immediately at website1082018@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete such information from our records.
