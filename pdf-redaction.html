Complete User Guide to PDF Redaction, Visual Masking, and Flattened Document Export

Why Flattened Export Matters When Redacting Sensitive PDF Information

Sharing contracts, invoices, statements, medical records, or identity forms often requires hiding confidential details such as account numbers, phone numbers, addresses, or signatures. Simply placing a loose shape over text in a PDF editor can sometimes leave underlying text objects selectable or extractable unless the page is properly flattened. By rendering PDF pages to a high-resolution canvas, painting your chosen redaction rectangles directly onto the page bitmap, and embedding the resulting flattened page images into a downloadable PDF (or PNG), this tool produces a clean, self-contained output without transmitting your confidential document to any remote server. A browser-based PDF Redaction tool runs 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

How to Redact a PDF in 3 Easy Steps

  1. Choose Your Local PDF File: Click Choose PDF to open a document from your device—everything stays in local browser memory.
  2. Select Page & Drag Redaction Boxes: Enter the Page Number to inspect, choose a fill style (solid black, charcoal, or white), and drag rectangles over the areas you want to cover. Use Undo Box or Clear Page Boxes anytime.
  3. Download Redacted PDF or Page PNG: Click Download Redacted PDF to save a flattened PDF of your pages with redactions baked in, or Download Page PNG for the active page.

Normalized Box Coordinates Across Pages

Each redaction rectangle is stored relative to the page dimensions (0.0 to 1.0 in width and height) so boxes stay aligned across pages:

Normalized X = x_px / pageWidth_px  |  Normalized Y = y_px / pageHeight_px
Flattened Output = Rendered Page + Painted Redaction Rectangles → Embedded into PDF / PNG

Helpful Tips Before Sharing a Redacted Document

  • Inspect Every Redacted Box Visually Before Sharing: Confirm that every character, digit, or mark you intended to hide is completely covered with a slight margin around edges.
  • Keep an Unredacted Original for Your Own Records: Save your unredacted source PDF in a secure folder before distributing the redacted copy.
  • Understand Flattened Image PDFs: Flattening converts pages into raster images embedded in a PDF, so text in the exported file is no longer selectable text.

Worked Example: Open a 3-page invoice PDF, go to Page 1, drag a black box over the account number and another over a phone number, switch to Page 2 to mask a billing address, and click Download Redacted PDF to save the flattened result locally.
