How MediaPipe AI Background Remover With 1.5px Edge Smoothing Works

Standard color-threshold tools often struggle when a photo has mixed lighting or textured surroundings. This tool uses MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation in the browser to separate the subject from the background in a few seconds, then applies a blur(1.5px) edge-smoothing pass to the mask so edges look natural rather than jagged.
