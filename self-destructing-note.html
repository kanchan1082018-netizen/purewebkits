How Self-Destructing Private Notes, Secure Sharing & One-Time Deletion Work

### Why Self-Destructing Private Notes Are Essential for Secure Communication

Sharing sensitive information such as API keys, passwords, financial tokens, or confidential instructions via standard chat apps or email leaves a permanent digital footprint vulnerable to archiving and interception. A self-destructing private note ensures that once the recipient opens the link and reads the message, it is permanently purged from storage, preventing unauthorized secondary access.
