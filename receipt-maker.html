Complete User Guide to Creating Itemized Receipts, Tax/Discount Totals & PDF/Text Exports

How Itemized Line Totals, Discounts, Tax & Grand Totals Are Calculated

A clear, itemized receipt helps freelancers, small shops, tutors, and service providers record transactions transparently and give customers a clean record of payment. Each line item multiplies quantity (Qty) by unit price (Price) to form line totals, which sum into the Subtotal. Applying any flat Discount and then calculating percentage Tax on the discounted taxable amount (max(0, Subtotal − Discount)) produces a consistent, easy-to-audit Grand Total. You can print or save the formatted receipt sheet as a PDF directly from your browser or export a plain-text copy for bookkeeping logs. A browser-based Receipt Maker runs 100% locally in your browser—with zero server uploads.

How to Create a Receipt in 3 Easy Steps

  2. Add or Remove Itemized Line Items: Enter an item or service description, quantity, and unit price and click + Add (or remove any row you no longer need).
  3. Print/Save PDF or Export Plain Text: Click Print / Save PDF to open your browser's print dialog, or click Copy Receipt Text / Download .txt.

Receipt Subtotal, Discount & Tax Formula

For line items i, flat discount D, and tax rate t = tax% / 100:

Subtotal = Σ(Qtyi × Unit_Pricei)  |  Taxable = max(0, Subtotal − D)
Tax Amount = Taxable × t  |  Grand Total = Taxable + Tax_Amount

Helpful Tips for Clean Receipt Records

  • Use Unique Sequential Receipt Numbers: Labeling receipts cleanly (such as RCT-2026-104) makes matching payments and records much easier later.
  • Verify Tax Rules for Your Jurisdiction: Tax applicability and rates depend on your location and goods/services—confirm the right rate for your transaction.
  • Save a Copy Locally Before Closing: Because this tool runs client-side with no cloud database, export a PDF or .txt copy if you need a permanent record.

Worked Example: Two line items totaling a $120.00 subtotal with a $5.00 discount give a $115.00 taxable base; at 8.0% tax ($9.20), the grand total is $124.20.
