### PDF Coordinate Stamping Formula + Worked Example

Page numbering tools calculate exact coordinate placement on PDF pages using dimensional points measured from bottom-left origins:

$\text{Bottom Center X} = \frac{\text{Page Width}}{2}  |  \text{Footer Y} = 30\text{ points}$

Worked Example: Stamping page numbers onto an **A4 PDF document** (width 595.28 points) at the **bottom center footer** calculates exact horizontal placement at $595.28 / 2 = 297.6$ points with a vertical margin of 30 points from the bottom edge.
