How International Paper Sizes, ISO 216 Standards & Print Resolutions Work

### Why Paper Size Guides Are Essential for Designers and Printers

In publishing, graphic design, and document printing, knowing exact paper dimensions is vital for avoiding misaligned layouts and cropped margins. Globally, paper sizes are standardized by the International Organization for Standardization (**ISO 216**), most notably the **A-Series** (where A4 is the universal standard for office documents). Meanwhile, North America relies on imperial dimensions such as **US Letter** and **Legal**. An **international paper size guide** converts measurements effortlessly between millimeters, inches, and high-resolution printing pixels (300 DPI). Our browser-based tool computes dimensions locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Use the Paper Size Guide in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Select Paper Format: Choose from standard ISO A-Series (A0 to A6) or North American sizes (Letter, Legal, Tabloid).
  2. Choose Orientation: Toggle between Portrait (vertical) and Landscape (horizontal) layouts.
  3. Inspect Dimensions: Instantly review exact measurements in millimeters, inches, and pixels at 300 DPI.
### Conversion Formula + Worked Example

Paper dimensions convert from millimeters to inches by dividing by 25.4, and pixels are calculated by multiplying inches by print resolution (DPI):

Inches = Millimeters / 25.4  |  Pixels (300 DPI) = Inches * 300

Worked Example: A standard ISO A4 sheet measures 210 x 297 mm (8.27 x 11.69 inches), which translates to **2,480 x 3,508 pixels** at standard 300 DPI high-resolution printing quality.
