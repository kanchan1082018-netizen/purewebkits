How Tip Calculations, Hospitality Gratuities & Bill Splitting Work

### Why Restaurant Tip & Bill Split Calculators Are Essential

Tipping is a customary practice in restaurants, cafes, and hospitality services to reward waitstaff for their service quality. When dining out with friends or family, splitting the total check (including tax and tip) evenly among participants can often become tedious. Our browser-based Tip & Restaurant Bill Split Calculator computes individual shares and gratuities instantly in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate Tips and Split Bills in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Bill Amount: Type your restaurant subtotal or total check amount into the bill amount input field.
  2. Select Tip %: Choose a preset tip button (10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%) or type a custom tip percentage.
  3. Split Bill: Enter the number of people sharing the bill to instantly see each person's exact share.
### Tip & Bill Splitting Mathematical Formulas + Worked Example

Hospitality tip computations and per-person division use standard proportional arithmetic:

Tip Amount = Bill * (Tip % / 100)  |  Total Due = Bill + Tip  |  Per Person = Total / People

Worked Example: A $120.00 restaurant bill with an 18% tip yields $21.60 in gratuity, making the total $141.60. Split among 3 people, each person pays $47.20.
