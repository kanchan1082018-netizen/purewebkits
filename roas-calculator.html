How Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), CPA & Marketing Profitability Work

### Why ROAS & CPA Calculators Are Essential for E-commerce & Marketers

Evaluating the financial performance of paid digital advertising campaigns on Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, or Amazon requires tracking metrics that go beyond simple click counts. **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)** measures the gross revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising, while **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)** reveals how much it costs to acquire a paying customer. A ROAS & CPA calculator computes these critical performance ratios instantly. Our browser-based tool calculates marketing profitability locally in client memory without server uploads.

### How to Calculate ROAS in 3 Easy Steps
  1. Enter Total Ad Spend: Input your total advertising budget expended during the campaign.
  2. Enter Revenue Generated: Type the total gross revenue earned directly from your ad campaigns.
  3. Enter Conversions: Input total customer acquisitions to inspect your CPA and net profit immediately.
### Calculation Formulas + Worked Example

ROAS divides total revenue by total ad spend, while CPA divides total spend by total conversions:

ROAS (Ratio) = Total Revenue / Total Ad Spend  |  CPA = Total Ad Spend / Total Conversions

Worked Example: Spending $2,000 on ads to generate $8,000 in revenue across 40 customer conversions yields a **4.00x ROAS (400%)**, a **$50.00 CPA**, and a net advertising profit of **+$6,000**.
